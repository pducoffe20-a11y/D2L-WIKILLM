# Value Model - Platform Consolidation

## Use when

The buyer may retire learning platforms, point solutions, infrastructure, integrations or duplicated operating processes.

## Cost inventory

Model current and proposed states using the same categories:

- Subscriptions and support.
- Hosting and infrastructure.
- Integration build, monitoring and repair.
- Identity and data operations.
- Administration and reporting labor.
- Content and accessibility tooling.
- External services and contractors.
- Security, audit and vendor-management effort.
- Migration, archival and transition costs.
- Training, change and adoption.
- Residual tools that cannot be retired.

## Formula

```text
Recurring gross consolidation benefit =
  retired current-state annual cost
  - proposed incremental annual cost

Net consolidation value =
  recurring gross consolidation benefit
  + validated cashable labor savings
  - one-time migration, integration, content and change cost
```

Use a multiyear cash-flow model when one-time transition cost is material.

## Discovery

- Which products and costs will actually be retired?
- Which capabilities must remain as integrations or specialist tools?
- When do current contracts end?
- What data or records must remain accessible?
- Which internal teams perform hidden integration and reconciliation work?
- What new responsibilities appear in the proposed state?

## Proof

[[Proof Card - Year Up United - Consolidation and Administrative Efficiency]] reports consolidation of three LMS platforms and measured workflow savings. It does not establish the buyer's license savings, migration effort or target architecture.

## Claims to avoid

- One platform replaces every point solution.
- Fewer vendors always lowers total cost or risk.
- Current contract values or renewal dates can be inferred.
- Administrative efficiency counted here is also added in [[Value Model - Administrative Efficiency]].
