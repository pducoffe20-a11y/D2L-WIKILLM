## Calculation

Write the formula in words and symbols. Show units so incompatible values cannot be multiplied accidentally.

```text
Gross benefit = validated volume change × validated unit value

Realized benefit = gross benefit × realization factor

Net benefit = realized benefit - incremental cost
```

The realization factor represents adoption, ramp, process change and attribution. It is not a hidden discount for uncertainty; explain its components.
