# Objection - Migration Risk


    ## Seller truth

    Acknowledge risk, define the migration scope, and identify who must validate the plan.

    ## Why it matters

    An objection response should reduce uncertainty and advance discovery.

    ## When to use it

    Live buyer conversation, follow-up, or proposal review.

    ## Evidence

    Batch 1 did not include current migration methodology or services scope. Treat migration planning, effort, sequencing, and ownership as unresolved until the services batch is reviewed.

    ## Seller interpretation

    **Interpretation:** The objection may be a request for evidence, process clarity, or risk ownership.

    ## Talk track

    That’s a fair concern. Could we define what would make this risk acceptable for your team?

    ## Discovery questions

    - What specifically worries you?
- What evidence would help?
- Who needs to be comfortable with the answer?

    ## Objections and cautions

    **Behind it (hypothesis):** data loss, disruption, hidden dependencies or stakeholder resistance.
    **Diagnose:** What content, data, integrations and continuity requirements are non-negotiable?
    **Use:** current migration and implementation evidence; do not promise zero disruption or a timeline.
    **Next step:** scope a migration inventory and risk review.
