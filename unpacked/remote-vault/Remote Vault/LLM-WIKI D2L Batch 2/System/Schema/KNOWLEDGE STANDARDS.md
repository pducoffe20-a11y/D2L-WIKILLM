# Knowledge Standards

Every curated note uses the universal schema and separates source fact from seller interpretation. Status is explicit: `inbox`, `reviewed`, `verified`, `disputed`, `stale`, or `archived`.

## Promotion gates

- `inbox → reviewed`: structure, ownership, source, confidentiality, deduplication, and links checked.
- `reviewed → verified`: the note satisfies one declared verification scope, has a verification basis, and has a separate external-use posture.
- Approved SharePoint sales-library content may be verified as `approved-library` without sentence-level claim extraction.
- Seller guidance may be verified when its sources are approved, its interpretation is labeled, and its boundaries are preserved.
- Record confirmation and gap confirmation are valid verification outcomes; neither upgrades every embedded claim.
- Any contradiction: keep both records and mark `conflicts-with`; do not choose silently.
- Low confidence does not prevent verification. It describes support strength within the declared scope.

## Seller response labels

Use **Known**, **Signal**, **Interpretation**, **Missing**, **Stale**, **Risk**, and **Next check**.
