# Source - FY27 Winning Story Association Focus

## Seller truth

Association positioning should connect the buyer’s operating model and growth priorities to evidence that is relevant to that association’s context.

## Evidence

- The deck emphasizes association growth, ease of use, insight, partnership, and transformation as positioning themes.
- It presents a REINSW outcome statement as a customer proof candidate.

## Objections and cautions

- These themes are hypotheses for discovery, not facts about a specific association.
- Keep the REINSW outcome scoped to that customer and validate it against the underlying approved customer source before external reuse.
- Do not generalize satisfaction, usage, growth, or transformation outcomes.
