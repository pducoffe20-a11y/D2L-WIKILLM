# Accessibility Plus Current Internal Status

## Known

The current internal sources separate two things:

1. **Accessibility Advisory Services** — a services-led offer available now.
2. **Accessibility+ product solution** — a future product direction still in development.

The services-led offer combines automated scanning and course-level reporting with human review, prioritization, consultative guidance and optional scoped remediation support.

## Current service boundaries

- The current source describes English-language scanning and remediation.
- Scanning covers supported Brightspace content types described in the source; do not generalize it to all third-party content.
- Copyright or third-party ownership can limit remediation.
- The service does not guarantee complete remediation, accessibility compliance or a legal outcome.
- Remediation scope must be explicitly agreed.

## Future-product boundary

The future Accessibility+ product capabilities and timing remain subject to change. Internal and SharePoint sources explicitly treat roadmap timing as nonbinding. Do not present planned product functionality or timing as generally available or contracted.

## Seller decision

Position the current services-led workflow only after confirming:

- content types and volume;
- active-course priorities;
- language;
- internal accessibility expertise;
- desired report, consultation and remediation scope;
- who owns legal and compliance interpretation.
