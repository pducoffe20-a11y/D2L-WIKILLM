# Proof Card - SAIT - Analytics-Led LMS Adoption

## Context

Southern Alberta Institute of Technology wanted Brightspace to function as a more consistent hub for students. The primary adoption challenge was faculty use, including duplicated grade-entry work.

## Buyer problem

Improve LMS adoption with evidence, workflow improvements, and peer support rather than treating adoption as a communications problem alone.

## What changed

SAIT combined Data Hub baselining and measurement, senior-leader goal setting, a 60-person faculty community of practice, subscription training, and integration between Brightspace and Ellucian Banner.

## Reported outcome

- Instructor engagement increased from 58% to 73% in 18 months: 15 percentage points.
- Instructors entering grades in Brightspace increased from 38% in 2015 to 88%.
- One instructor reported at least a 30% reduction in time spent managing classes, marking assignments, and preparing lessons.

## Safe claim

SAIT reports a 15-percentage-point increase in instructor engagement over 18 months after combining analytics, workflow integration, leadership goals, and a faculty community of practice.

## Do not overstate

- Do not attribute the adoption change to analytics or Brightspace alone.
- The 30% workload reduction is one instructor’s reported experience, not an institution-wide measured average.
- Do not convert a reported correlation between LMS use and student success into proof that LMS activity causes graduation or retention.
- Do not assume the same integration is available without technical validation.

## Best fit

- Higher education LMS adoption
- Change-management planning
- Data Hub and analytics discussions
- Prospects with duplicated SIS or ERP workflows

## Discovery questions

- Which roles and activities define meaningful adoption at your institution?
- What baseline data can distinguish access, activity, and effective teaching practice?
- Which duplicated workflows make instructors resist the platform?
- Who can form a credible peer community of practice?
