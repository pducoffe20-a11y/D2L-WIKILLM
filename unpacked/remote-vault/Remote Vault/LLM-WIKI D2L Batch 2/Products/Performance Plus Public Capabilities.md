# Performance Plus Public Capabilities

Performance+ is presented as an add-on package for Brightspace.

## Leadership

- Adoption Dashboard for Brightspace usage data.
- Engagement Dashboard for course performance and trends.
- Visual Diagnostics for comparisons across key metrics.

## Administrators

- Create, edit and share custom dashboards and reports.
- Refresh data used in visualizations.
- Schedule reports, alerts and updates.
- Control access to reports and visualizations.

## Educators

- Predictive analytics for identifying learners who may need attention.
- Pattern recognition across learner and course data.
- Assessment Quality Dashboard for question, score and response analysis.
- Learner Engagement Dashboard for individual learner metrics.

## Additional public offering

The page describes Analytics Builder Essentials as curated, ready-to-deploy dashboards focused on engagement, adoption and achievement. D2L Learning Analytics services are positioned as support for data strategy, reporting and ongoing analysis.

## Boundaries

- Performance+ is an add-on, not a blanket Brightspace Core claim.
- The page does not establish customer-specific ROI, savings or intervention outcomes.
