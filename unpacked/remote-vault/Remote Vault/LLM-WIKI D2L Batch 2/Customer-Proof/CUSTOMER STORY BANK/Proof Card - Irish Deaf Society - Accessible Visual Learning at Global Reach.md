# Proof Card - Irish Deaf Society - Accessible Visual Learning at Global Reach

## Context

The Irish Deaf Society (IDS) is the primary education provider for Ireland's Deaf community, most of whom communicate in Irish Sign Language (ISL). In 2022 IDS launched a digital learning experience on Brightspace Core to widen geographic reach and deliver visual, sign-language-first content.

## Buyer problem

A niche nonprofit serving a language-and-accessibility-specific community needs to extend reach beyond its home geography without compromising the visual, sign-language nature of its instruction.

## What changed

IDS built a centralized digital learning experience with visual course content and video resources designed for ISL instruction, moving beyond in-person-only delivery.

## Reported outcome

- Expanded learner reach internationally, including learners in the US, Australia, and Spain.
- Delivered a simpler, centralized experience for both administrators and learners.
- Supported visual learning specifically for courses taught in sign language.
- Published 400 language videos so members can learn between live lessons.

## Safe claim

The Irish Deaf Society reports that its Brightspace-based platform extended its reach from Ireland to learners in the US, Australia, and Spain while supporting a visual, sign-language-first course design with 400 supporting language videos.

## Do not overstate

- Do not claim broad accessibility certification beyond what's published for this specific case.
- Do not generalize "400 videos" as a required content volume for similar deployments.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Nonprofits and associations serving accessibility-specific or niche language communities
- Organizations needing visual/video-first course design rather than text-heavy content
- Small associations aiming to expand from a single country to international reach

- Does your learner community have specific accessibility or language needs that current tools don't serve well?
- How much of your content today is video/visual versus text, and does your platform support that well?
