# Source - Corporate Use Cases

## Seller truth

External and extended-enterprise learning requirements must be categorized by training model, audience, administration, outcomes, reporting, systems of record, and ecommerce before solution fit is asserted.

## Evidence

- The page distinguishes course completion, certification/qualification, and talent/skills models.
- It distinguishes internal, external, and extended-enterprise delivery.
- It warns that Brightspace alone may not usually meet several external, certification, skills, or distributed-administration patterns and may require another system of record or partner solution.
