# Proof Card - Grain and Feed Trade Association - Global Distance Learning at Scale

## Context

Gafta (Grain and Feed Trade Association) is a trade association whose membership spans more than 95 countries. Face-to-face training could not reach most members, so Gafta rebuilt its distance learning program on Brightspace Core with D2L Optimization Services.

## Buyer problem

A globally distributed membership needs consistent, credible education, but travel and time-zone barriers make in-person delivery impractical, and manual administration doesn't scale to a worldwide audience.

## What changed

Gafta moved its distance learning program onto Brightspace, automating registration and certificate distribution and consolidating traditional distance learning with new short courses on one platform.

## Reported outcome

- Automated course registration and assignment workflows, saving staff hours.
- Intelligent Agents send instant notifications so learners stay on schedule.
- Release Conditions removed the manual process of distributing course certificates.
- Reduced support requests through a more intuitive learner experience.
- One platform now supports both long-form distance learning and short courses.

## Safe claim

Gafta reports that Brightspace let it automate registration and certification workflows for a membership spread across more than 95 countries, reducing manual admin and support load while unifying its course catalog on one platform.

## Do not overstate

- Do not cite specific hours saved or support-ticket reduction percentages — none were published.
- Do not generalize the automation gains to organizations with different administrative workflows.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Global or multi-region trade and professional associations
- Membership organizations with administratively heavy certification/registration processes
- Associations consolidating multiple course formats (long courses + short courses) onto one platform

## Discovery questions

- How many countries or regions does your membership span, and how much of your training still requires in-person attendance?
- How much staff time goes into manual registration, notifications, or certificate distribution today?
- Are you running separate systems for different course formats (long-form vs. short courses)?
