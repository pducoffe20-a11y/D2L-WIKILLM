# Content Pathways - Recommended Reading Sequences

This page outlines recommended paths through the vault for different use cases. Use these to:
- Know where to start when researching a topic
- Understand logical information flow
- Build comprehensive context efficiently
- Avoid missing critical information

---

## Pathway Overview

Each pathway follows a progression:
1. **Foundation** - Core concepts and definitions
2. **Context** - Industry, market, competitive landscape
3. **Details** - Specific products, use cases, features
4. **Proof** - Customer evidence and case studies
5. **Application** - How to use this knowledge

---

## Sales Pathways

### Pathway 1: "I Need Customer Proof for a Prospect"

**Goal**: Find relevant customer success stories for a specific prospect  
**Time**: 10-15 minutes  
**Best For**: Sales conversations, proposal support, deal acceleration

#### Path A: If You Know Customer's Industry

1. **Start**: [[03-Industries/]] - Browse your prospect's industry
2. **Explore**: Find industry overview and market context
3. **Customers**: Navigate to customer stories in that industry
4. **Story Selection**: Review 2-3 customers of similar size/profile
5. **Details**: Read case studies and success metrics
6. **Differentiation**: Check competitive context (products used vs. alternatives)

**Representative Flow**:  
`Industry Research` → `Customer List by Industry` → `Select Similar Customer` → `Read Case Study` → `Extract Key Metrics`

#### Path B: If You Know Customer's Product Focus

1. **Start**: [[MOC]] - Review product capabilities
2. **Use Cases**: Explore what use cases the product enables
3. **Customer Examples**: Find customers who implemented similar use cases
4. **Story Selection**: Compare relevant customer stories
5. **Value Demonstration**: Extract benefits and outcomes

**Representative Flow**:  
`Product Overview` → `Use Cases Enabled` → `Customer Implementations` → `Case Studies` → `Value Metrics`

#### Path C: Quick Lookup by Customer

1. **Go To**: [[Customer Story Navigator]]
2. **Find**: Search customer by name
3. **Read**: Review their story and results
4. **Related**: Check similar customers and products
5. **Competitive**: See if competitive alternatives mentioned

**Representative Flow**:  
`Customer Navigator` → `Find Customer` → `Read Story` → `Explore Related` → `Competitive Context`

---

### Pathway 2: "I Need to Understand Our Competitive Position"

**Goal**: Understand how D2L compares to competitors in market  
**Time**: 20-30 minutes  
**Best For**: Competitive positioning, RFP response, strategy discussions

#### Steps

1. **Start**: [[06-Competitors/MOC.md]] - Competitive landscape overview
2. **Identify**: Find primary competitors in your segment
3. **Features**: Review feature comparison and capabilities
4. **Positioning**: Understand differentiation and win themes
5. **Customer Proof**: Find customers who chose D2L over competitors
6. **Win-Loss**: Review win-loss analysis if available
7. **Gaps**: Identify areas for improvement or messaging

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Competitive Overview` → `Identify Key Competitors` → `Feature Comparison` → `Win-Loss Analysis` → `Differentiation Themes` → `Customer Proof` → `Strategic Positioning`

---

### Pathway 3: "I'm Preparing for a Customer Call"

**Goal**: Get comprehensive background on customer and opportunity  
**Time**: 15-20 minutes (if customer in vault)  
**Best For**: Call prep, relationship building, opportunity assessment

#### Steps

1. **Customer Background**: [[Customer Story Navigator]] - Find and read customer story
2. **Industry Context**: [[03-Industries/]] - Understand their industry
3. **Products**: [[MOC]] - Review products relevant to their business
4. **Use Cases**: [[02 - Use-Cases MOC]] - Understand problems they solve
5. **Success Metrics**: From customer story - what outcomes matter?
6. **Competitive Context**: [[06-Competitors/MOC.md]] - Any competitive alternatives?
7. **Related Customers**: Find similar customers for case study references

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Customer Story` → `Industry Overview` → `Products & Capabilities` → `Relevant Use Cases` → `Success Metrics` → `Competitive Landscape` → `Similar Customer Stories`

---

## Product Pathways

### Pathway 4: "I Need to Understand Product Capabilities"

**Goal**: Comprehensive view of a product's features and applications  
**Time**: 20-25 minutes  
**Best For**: Product learning, feature explanation, capability discussion

#### Steps

1. **Overview**: [[MOC]] - Start with product index
2. **Features**: Review product feature list and capabilities
3. **Use Cases**: [[02 - Use-Cases MOC]] - What problems does it solve?
4. **Benefits**: Review value propositions and benefits
5. **Industries**: Which industries use it most?
6. **Customers**: [[05-Customer-Proof/]] - Find customer implementations
7. **Case Studies**: Read 2-3 customer stories with detailed use
8. **Roadmap**: Check for product strategy or upcoming features

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Product Overview` → `Feature Set` → `Use Cases` → `Industry Applications` → `Customer Implementations` → `Case Studies` → `Success Outcomes`

---

### Pathway 5: "I Want to Learn About a Specific Use Case"

**Goal**: Deep understanding of a particular use case across industries and products  
**Time**: 20-30 minutes  
**Best For**: Use case selling, industry-specific messaging, capability demonstration

#### Steps

1. **Definition**: [[02 - Use-Cases MOC]] - Understand the use case
2. **Industries**: Which industries prioritize this use case?
3. **Products**: What D2L products enable this use case?
4. **Benefits**: What outcomes does it deliver?
5. **Customers**: [[05-Customer-Proof/]] - Find customers who implemented it
6. **Metrics**: What success metrics are relevant?
7. **Competitors**: How do competitors approach this use case?
8. **ROI**: What's the value proposition and business case?

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Use Case Definition` → `Industry Application` → `Enabling Products` → `Benefits & Outcomes` → `Customer Examples` → `Success Metrics` → `Competitive Comparison` → `ROI/Business Case`

---

## Industry Pathways

### Pathway 6: "I Need an Industry Overview"

**Goal**: Complete understanding of an industry segment and our position  
**Time**: 25-35 minutes  
**Best For**: New industry territory, market research, strategic planning

#### Steps

1. **Industry Research**: [[03-Industries/]] - Browse industry section
2. **Market Overview**: Understand industry dynamics and trends
3. **Key Players**: Identify major customers and decision-makers
4. **Use Cases**: [[02 - Use-Cases MOC]] - What problems matter in this industry?
5. **Products**: [[MOC]] - What products apply?
6. **Customer Proof**: [[05-Customer-Proof/]] - Review industry-specific case studies
7. **Competitive Position**: [[06-Competitors/MOC.md]] - How do we compete in this industry?
8. **Opportunity Assessment**: Identify gaps and growth areas

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Industry Overview` → `Market Dynamics` → `Key Players` → `Relevant Use Cases` → `Applicable Products` → `Customer Evidence` → `Competitive Positioning` → `Opportunity Analysis`

## Learning & Development Pathways

### Pathway 7: "I'm New to D2L - What Should I Learn First?"

**Goal**: Get up to speed on D2L products, market, and positioning  
**Time**: 60-90 minutes (full overview)  
**Best For**: New team member onboarding, comprehensive learning

#### Steps

1. **Start**: [[VAULT DASHBOARD]] - Vault orientation
2. **Navigation**: [[Navigation - MOC/00-Start Here.md]] - Understand vault structure
3. **Products**: [[MOC]] - Learn D2L product suite
4. **Use Cases**: [[02 - Use-Cases MOC]] - Understand problems we solve
5. **Industries**: [[03-Industries/]] - Review key market segments
6. **Customers**: [[05-Customer-Proof/]] - Browse representative customer stories
7. **Competitive**: [[06-Competitors/MOC.md]] - Understand market position
8. **Deep Dives**: Pick 2-3 topics for deeper exploration

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Orientation` → `Navigation Guide` → `Product Overview` → `Use Cases` → `Industries` → `Customer Stories` → `Competitive Position` → `Deeper Learning`

### Pathway 8: "I'm Preparing a Presentation - Where's the Info?"

**Goal**: Gather relevant content for a specific topic or audience  
**Time**: 20-40 minutes depending on presentation scope  
**Best For**: Creating slides, marketing collateral, webinar content

#### Steps

1. **Topic Definition**: Clarify the presentation topic and audience
2. **Related MOC**: [[MOC]], [[02-Use-Cases/MOC.md]], etc. - Find relevant MOC
3. **Outline**: Review MOC structure for presentation flow
4. **Content**: Gather facts, statistics, customer stories
5. **Proof Points**: Find customer quotes and metrics
6. **Competitive**: Add competitive context if relevant
7. **Visuals**: Note any diagrams or relationship maps to recreate

**Recommended Sequence**:  
`Identify Topic MOC` → `Review Structure` → `Gather Facts` → `Proof Points` → `Competitive Context` → `References`

## Optimized Pathways by Time Available

### If You Have 5 Minutes
1. Quick lookup: [[Customer Story Navigator]]
2. Search for specific customer or topic
3. Read case study summary

### If You Have 10 Minutes
1. **Path 1**: Customer proof pathway (Path A or B)
2. **Path 2**: One MOC review (products, industries, or use cases)

### If You Have 20 Minutes
1. Start with relevant MOC
2. Explore related content via links
3. Read 1-2 case studies
4. Check competitive context

### If You Have 30+ Minutes
1. Choose a comprehensive pathway from above
2. Follow all steps
3. Take notes and build understanding

## Smart Pathway Selection

**Use this decision tree to pick your pathway**:

```
What's your main goal?
├─ Sales/Deal Support
│  ├─ Have customer name? → Pathway 3 (Call Prep)
│  ├─ Have industry? → Pathway 1 (Customer Proof)
│  ├─ Need competitive info? → Pathway 2 (Competition)
│  └─ Have product focus? → Pathway 5 (Use Case)
├─ Product Learning
│  ├─ Learning specific product? → Pathway 4 (Product Overview)
│  ├─ Learning specific use case? → Pathway 5 (Use Case)
│  └─ New to D2L? → Pathway 7 (Comprehensive Learning)
├─ Industry Research
│  └─ Need industry overview? → Pathway 6 (Industry Overview)
├─ Presentation Prep
│  └─ Creating presentation? → Pathway 8 (Gather Content)
└─ Staying Current
   └─ What's new? → Pathway 9 (Recent Updates)
```
