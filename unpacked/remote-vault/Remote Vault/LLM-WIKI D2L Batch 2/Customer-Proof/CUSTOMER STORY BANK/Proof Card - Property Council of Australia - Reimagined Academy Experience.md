# Proof Card - Property Council of Australia - Reimagined Academy Experience

## Context

The Property Council of Australia runs a member-facing training academy for its ~1,500 real-estate industry learners, rebuilt on Brightspace Core to modernize the learning experience.

## Buyer problem

An association's existing "academy" or member-training platform feels outdated to members, limiting engagement with content that could otherwise incorporate video, interactivity, and gamification.

## What changed

Property Council of Australia rebuilt its academy on Brightspace Core, giving members an intuitive, easy-to-navigate experience that supports richer content formats.

## Reported outcome

- Members describe the platform as intuitive and user-friendly.
- Easier navigation improved how members engage with content.
- Content delivery now incorporates video, interactive elements, and gamification.

## Safe claim

Property Council of Australia reports that rebuilding its academy on Brightspace made the experience more intuitive for its ~1,500 members and enabled richer content formats, including video, interactivity, and gamification.

## Do not overstate

- No quantified engagement or satisfaction metric was published for this story — do not invent a percentage.
- Do not claim this proves gamification alone drives outcomes; it was one of several format changes.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Trade and professional associations with an aging member-training "academy" platform
- Real estate, property, and similarly credential-driven industry associations
- Buyers whose core objection is member experience/UX, not administration

## Discovery questions

- What feedback have you heard from members about your current training platform's usability?
- Are you limited today in the content formats you can deliver (video, interactive, gamified)?
- How central is your "academy" brand to your association's member value proposition?
