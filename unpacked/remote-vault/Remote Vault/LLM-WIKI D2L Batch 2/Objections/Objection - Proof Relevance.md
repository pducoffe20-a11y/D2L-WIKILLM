# Objection - Proof Relevance


    ## Seller truth

    Use only proof that matches the buyer’s problem, context, and outcome.

    ## Why it matters

    An objection response should reduce uncertainty and advance discovery.

    ## When to use it

    Live buyer conversation, follow-up, or proposal review.

    ## Evidence

    - FY27 Winning Story enablement instructs sellers to match customer proof to the buyer’s geography, industry, challenge, and desired outcome.
    - It directs sellers to validate customer metrics against the underlying approved metrics table before external sharing.

    ## Seller interpretation
