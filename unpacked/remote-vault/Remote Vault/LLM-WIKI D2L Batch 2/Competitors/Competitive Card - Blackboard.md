# Competitive Card - Blackboard

## Governance

- **Current approved guidance:** [May 2026 D2L vs Blackboard battlecard](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Education/INTERNAL-MAY2026-D2LvsBlackboard-Battlecard-.pptx?web=1). It proposes evaluation around AI breadth, analytics, outcomes, services, personalization and migration/change concerns.
- **Historical intelligence:** older Blackboard comparisons and service scans are not current.
- **Automated competitive signal:** [Blackboard's official product page](https://www.anthology.com/products/teaching-and-learning/learning-effectiveness/blackboard), captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30, now emphasizes core AI, accessibility, personalized learning, institutional controls, security and adjacent products.
- **Material conflict:** the May battlecard characterizes Blackboard AI as narrow and relies on restructuring/innovation claims. Blackboard's current page presents multiple core AI capabilities plus a premium AVA offering. Do not use the battlecard's AI-scope, hallucination-rate, roadmap, analytics-latency or company-direction assertions without current feature-level proof.

## Where they fit

Blackboard can fit education institutions that value platform familiarity, recent Ultra investment, core teaching workflows, integrated AI claims and adjacent Blackboard offerings. Existing adoption and change fatigue may be as important as feature fit.

## Likely buyer rationale

- Faculty and administrators know the platform.
- The institution has already invested in Ultra migration, training or integrations.
- Included AI or adjacent Blackboard products appear to meet requirements.
- Avoiding another change is viewed as lower risk.

## Discovery questions

- What changed after the Ultra transition, and which goals remain unmet?
- Which AI workflows are active, governed and producing evidence of value?
- How quickly can instructors and leaders act on learner and outcomes data?
- Which accessibility, content-authoring and accreditation workflows are core versus adjacent products?
- Where does service remain technical support versus strategic adoption or instructional-design support?
- What would justify another migration for faculty and learners?

## Safe differentiation

- Validate Brightspace across the buyer's full workflow: creation, assessment, personalization, analytics, outcomes and intervention.
- Bring services, adoption and migration acceptance criteria into the evaluation.
- Ask the buyer to demonstrate current Blackboard workflows rather than relying on an old feature matrix.
- Position change around a validated strategic gap, not company speculation.

## Claims to avoid

- Blackboard is unstable, “catching up,” underinvesting or lacks a viable roadmap.
- Blackboard AI is limited, unsafe or has a stated hallucination rate without current approved proof.
- Analytics always lag by 24 hours or accreditation reporting is inadequate.
- Blackboard discounts, packaging or services behave a certain way in this deal.
- Faculty will prefer Brightspace or migration will be easy.

## Desired next step

Select the unresolved post-Ultra goals, agree on scored demonstrations and define what evidence would justify the cost and disruption of change.
