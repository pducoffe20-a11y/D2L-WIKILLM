# Proof Card - ETFO - Nine Years of Enrollment Growth in Union Professional Learning

## Context

The Elementary Teachers' Federation of Ontario (ETFO), a teachers' union, built online professional learning with Brightspace Core, Technical Support Services, and Learning Services to make additional-qualification courses accessible and affordable for members who can't commit to face-to-face training.

## Buyer problem

Members (teachers) can't always attend in-person training due to full-time work and family obligations, but still need accessible, cost-effective, and accredited professional learning to advance their careers.

## What changed

ETFO developed online professional learning options tailored to member needs and accessible cost, sustained over nearly a decade.

## Reported outcome

- First-year enrollment was three times higher than ETFO's targets (1,500 vs. 500 target).
- Steady enrollment increases for nine consecutive years.
- Recognized by the Ontario College of Teachers (OCT) for superior course development.
- More than 65,000 learners served over 10 years.

## Safe claim

ETFO reports that its Brightspace-based online professional learning program exceeded first-year enrollment targets threefold and grew steadily for nine consecutive years, serving more than 65,000 learners over a decade while earning outside recognition for course quality.

## Do not overstate

- Do not imply this growth trajectory is typical — this is a single, long-tenured customer example.
- Do not attribute the OCT recognition specifically to the platform rather than ETFO's course design.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Unions and member associations delivering accredited professional development
- K-12-adjacent training organizations proving multi-year program sustainability
- Buyers who need a long-track-record example, not just a launch-year success story

## Discovery questions

- What would sustained, multi-year enrollment growth be worth to your board versus a one-time launch spike?
- How accessible (cost and schedule) is your current professional learning offering to working members?
- Do you have a path to third-party recognition or accreditation for your courses today?
