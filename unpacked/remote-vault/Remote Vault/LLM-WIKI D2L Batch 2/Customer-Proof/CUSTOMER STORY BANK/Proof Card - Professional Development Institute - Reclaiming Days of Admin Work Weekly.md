# Proof Card - Professional Development Institute - Reclaiming Days of Admin Work Weekly

## Context

The Professional Development Institute (PDI), a small training organization serving K-12 teachers, built a flexible online platform on Brightspace Core with Partner Integration to help teachers fit professional development into busy schedules while meeting external accreditation requirements (UCSD Extension).

## Buyer problem

A lean training organization's team spends so much time on administration that it can't focus on customer service or growing the business, and needs to meet rigorous external course-approval standards.

## What changed

PDI moved course delivery and administration onto Brightspace, reclaiming significant staff time and enabling the team to meet UCSD Extension's course-approval requirements.

## Reported outcome

- Saves four days of administrative work per week.
- Frees the PDI team to focus on customer service and scaling the business.
- Provides a flexible online environment at an affordable price.
- Meets UCSD Extension's rigorous course-approval requirements.

## Safe claim

PDI reports that moving to Brightspace saved its team four days of administrative work per week, freeing staff to focus on customer service and business growth while still meeting UCSD Extension's course-approval requirements.

## Do not overstate

- "Four days per week" is a strong, specific claim — verify against the live page before quoting, since it implies a large share of a work-week.
- Do not assume this time-savings figure applies to organizations with different team sizes or admin loads.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Small training organizations and nonprofits with lean administrative teams
- Buyers whose core objection is administrative burden limiting growth capacity
- Organizations needing to meet external accreditation/course-approval standards

## Discovery questions

- How many days per week does your team currently spend on training administration versus growth activities?
- Do you have external accreditation or course-approval requirements you need your platform to support?
- What would your team do with four extra days a week?
