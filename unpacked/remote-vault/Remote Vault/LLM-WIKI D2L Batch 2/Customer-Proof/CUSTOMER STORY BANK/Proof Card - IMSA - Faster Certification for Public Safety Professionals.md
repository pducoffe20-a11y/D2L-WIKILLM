# Proof Card - IMSA - Faster Certification for Public Safety Professionals

## Context

The International Municipal Signals Association (IMSA) certifies public safety and infrastructure professionals. It moved certification training and exams onto Brightspace Core with partner integration to reduce the time and travel burden of career advancement for its ~9,300 learners.

## Buyer problem

Members need to earn or renew certifications without traveling or taking time off work, and the association needs a more efficient way to administer training, testing, and credentialing at scale.

## What changed

IMSA put certification study and exams online through Brightspace, letting members study and test anywhere, and streamlined the administrative workload for association staff.

## Reported outcome

- Reduced certification time for members.
- Members can study and take exams anywhere without travel or time off work.
- Streamlined administrative processes, freeing staff for more effective use of time.
- Unlocked new types of training so members can pass skills to the next generation.

## Safe claim

IMSA reports that moving certification onto Brightspace reduced certification time for its ~9,300 members by removing the need to travel for study or exams, while streamlining administrative work for staff.

## Do not overstate

- No specific percentage or time-saved figure was published — do not invent one.
- Do not claim this generalizes to all certification models without validating exam/proctoring requirements.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Certification and accreditation bodies for working professionals
- Associations where travel/scheduling is the main barrier to member advancement
- Public-safety-adjacent or infrastructure professional associations

## Discovery questions

- How much of your certification process currently requires in-person attendance or travel?
- What does your team spend the most administrative time on: scheduling, testing, or credential issuance?
- Are there new training or certification offerings you can't currently launch due to platform limitations?
