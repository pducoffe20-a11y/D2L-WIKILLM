# Proof Card - Epworth HealthCare - Compliance Learning Efficiency

## Context

Epworth HealthCare is a large not-for-profit healthcare group operating in a 24/7 clinical environment. It wanted to extend learning beyond compliance while reducing the cost and time burden of face-to-face and outsourced e-learning.

## Buyer problem

Maintain regulated training while making learning more engaging, faster to produce, and less dependent on scarce physical space and staff time.

## What changed

Epworth used Brightspace as part of an integrated learning environment spanning compliance, onboarding, professional development, communities of practice, video, surveys, and discussion.

## Reported outcome

- Learning costs reduced by 66%.
- E-learning modules and content delivered five times faster.
- Compliance-course duration reduced from 25 minutes to 10 minutes.
- Content-development costs reduced by 30%.
- Online training cost reported as 25% of face-to-face-focused training.

## Safe claim

Epworth HealthCare reports reducing learning costs by two-thirds and delivering e-learning content five times faster after changing its content-development and blended-learning model with D2L.

## Do not overstate

- Do not promise the same cost reduction or speed improvement for another healthcare organization.
- Do not imply that shorter compliance training is automatically more effective or compliant.
- Do not omit the prior outsourced-development baseline or the broader operating-model change.
- Do not use this older story as evidence of current product packaging, integration availability, or regulatory compliance.

## Best fit

- Healthcare and regulated training
- Compliance modernization
- Blended learning and content-production efficiency
- Buyers with high face-to-face delivery costs

## Discovery questions

- What portion of regulated learning requires live practice, and what can move online safely?
- What is the current cost and elapsed time to develop or update a module?
- How is learning effectiveness validated when course duration changes?
- Which existing compliance, video, survey, and collaboration systems must remain integrated?
