# Competitive Card - Moodle

## Governance

- **Current approved guidance:** [Brightspace-Moodle one-pager](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Education/Brightspace-Moodle_one-pager.pdf?web=1), updated 2026-06-11. It emphasizes partnership, centralized SaaS governance, content creation, measurable value and governed AI.
- **Historical intelligence:** the 2022 Moodle comparison and Moodle 4.0 release notes in the historical CI inventory are question sources only.
- **Automated competitive signal:** [Moodle's official LMS page](https://moodle.com/solutions/lms/), captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30, positions Moodle as secure, scalable, customizable, accessible and integration-friendly, with AI choice and control. It presents direct download, MoodleCloud and certified-service-provider routes.
- **Boundary:** “Moodle” is not one operating model. Hosting, version, partner, plugins, custom code and internal staffing materially change the comparison.

## Where they fit

Moodle can fit organizations that value open-source control, customization, community extensions or an established internal/partner operating model. It also offers hosted and service-provider routes. The actual deployment must be identified before positioning.

## Likely buyer rationale

- Preference for control, extensibility or an open-source model.
- Existing technical expertise, partner relationships or custom workflows.
- Belief that licensing economics are favorable.
- Desire to choose infrastructure, plugins and AI connections.
- Low appetite to replace a system that staff already maintain.

## Discovery questions

- Who hosts, upgrades, secures and supports the current instance?
- Which plugins and customizations are mission-critical, and who owns their lifecycle?
- What internal and partner effort is required during upgrades or incidents?
- How consistent are course design, accessibility and mobile experiences across programs?
- Where are content creation, analytics, credentials and AI governed?
- Which costs and staff time are included in the current total-cost view?

## Safe differentiation

- Compare responsibility models: centrally governed SaaS and D2L partnership versus the buyer's actual Moodle operating model.
- Demonstrate Brightspace content-authoring, automation, analytics and AI workflows against named requirements.
- Quantify administration and maintenance effort with the buyer; do not assume it.
- Use migration discovery to separate standard content, custom code, plugins, integrations and historical data.

## Claims to avoid

- Moodle is free, insecure, unreliable or unsupported.
- Every Moodle deployment depends on a reseller or disruptive annual upgrades.
- Plugins necessarily create a fragmented or inaccessible experience.
- Brightspace always costs less or produces higher ROI.
- A customer-specific open-source migration story proves the same outcome for this buyer.

## Desired next step

Document the current Moodle operating model and select one representative course, one custom/plugin workflow and one reporting workflow for side-by-side validation.
