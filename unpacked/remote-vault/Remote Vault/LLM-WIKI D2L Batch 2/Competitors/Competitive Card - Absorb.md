# Competitive Card - Absorb

## Governance

- **Current approved guidance:** the [July 2026 Financial Services ICP PMM Playbook](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Corporate/Financial%20Services%20PMM%20Playbook.pdf?web=1) positions Absorb around ease of use, fast deployment and external training delivery. It suggests differentiating Brightspace through enterprise-scale workforce development and future-skills initiatives. This guidance is financial-services scoped.
- **Historical intelligence:** the 2021 screenshots and 2022 Absorb comparison in the historical CI inventory are not approved current claims.
- **Automated competitive signal:** [Absorb's official page](https://www.absorblms.com/), captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30, presents an AI-powered enterprise LMS with employee development, content creation, compliance/ROI reporting, integrations and ecommerce.
- **Missing evidence:** no current dedicated D2L-vs-Absorb battlecard was found in the approved library.

## Where they fit

Absorb may fit corporate buyers seeking an enterprise LMS for employee or external training, with emphasis on ease, AI-enabled workflows, integrations, content creation or ecommerce. Confirm the actual audience and proposed product scope.

## Likely buyer rationale

- Fast time to launch and straightforward administration.
- One platform for employee, customer or partner learning.
- Interest in embedded AI, content creation or course commerce.
- Preference for a focused corporate LMS rather than a broader talent suite.

## Discovery questions

- Is the priority training administration, workforce capability, external education or a mix?
- How are role-based pathways, certifications and recurring requirements managed?
- What must managers and executives see beyond completions?
- How complex are delegated administration, audiences, integrations and commerce?
- What evidence is required to connect learning with readiness or performance?
- Which implementation and ongoing-service responsibilities matter?

## Safe differentiation

- In financial services, explore regulated learning, certification, role-based development and workforce-readiness outcomes.
- Demonstrate Brightspace against the buyer's audience, workflow and reporting requirements.
- Test depth and operating model; do not equate “focused” with “limited.”
- Use relevant D2L customer proof only after matching industry, use case and outcome.

## Claims to avoid

- Absorb lacks customization, accessibility, support, enterprise scale or reporting.
- Absorb is only for simple or external training.
- D2L deploys faster, costs less or produces stronger ROI.
- Vendor rankings, customer counts or AI claims from Absorb's site as independent proof.
- Applying financial-services positioning universally.

## Desired next step

Agree on a representative role-based program, a regulated/certification workflow and an executive-reporting scenario for validation.
