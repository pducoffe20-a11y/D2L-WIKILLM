# VP / Director of Education & Credentialing

## Role context

**Demographics & titles:**
- VP of Education
- Director of Certification & Credentialing
- VP of Professional Development
- Chief Learning Officer

**Target verticals:**
- Trade & Professional Associations
- Healthcare Credentialing Boards
- Financial Services Institutes

**Organizational scope:**
- Organization size: 5,000–100,000+ members; small-to-midsize internal team (3–10 staff)
- Reports to: CEO / Executive Director / Board of Directors

## Business drivers

**Primary objective:** Drive non-dues revenue via certification/CE programs, increase member engagement/retention, and simplify complex credentialing pathways.

**Key performance indicators (KPIs):**
- Non-dues revenue
- Course/certification completion rate
- Member retention rate
- Time-to-credential
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

**Business pressures:**
- Evolving industry regulations (medical compliance, financial standards)
- Competing with low-cost commercial aggregators
- Manual administrative strain with lean staffing

## Buying behavior

**Technical literacy:** Moderate; relies heavily on AMS/CRM integrations (Salesforce, Impexium, Personify, Higher Logic) and vendor support

**Decision role:** Primary Decision-Maker / Business Owner

**Budget authority:** Owns P&L for professional development ($50k–$100k+)

**Key objections & concerns:**
- Will this seamlessly integrate with our AMS/CRM?
- How difficult is migration for our existing courses?
- Is the interface intuitive enough for non-tech-savvy members?

## Discovery questions

- When members earn CE units or certifications with you today, what is the completion rate—and how much non-dues revenue is left on the table due to drop-offs?
- What percentage of member engagement comes from your education and certification programs?
- How many manual hours does your team spend on course setup, member enrollment, and credential issuance?

## D2L proof points

**D2L enablers:**
- Competency-based learning (CBL)
- Native badging & automated certificates
- Seamless AMS integrations
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance

**Relevant case studies:**
- [[ISC2 - 28% increase in course completion rates]]
- [[American Nurses Association (ANA) - 91% of learners reported improved clinical nursing practice]]
