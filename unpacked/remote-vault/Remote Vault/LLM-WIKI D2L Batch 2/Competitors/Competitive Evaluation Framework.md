# Competitive Evaluation Framework

## Createspace: verified scope and boundaries

The [Createspace Competitive Intelligence Scan](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Services/Createspace%20Competitive%20Scan%20Internal.pdf?web=1), updated 2026-06-30, positions Createspace as a Brightspace-native content-operations workspace for centralized creation, reuse and deployment.

The scan explicitly says Createspace is not an enterprise CMS, a replacement for specialist authoring tools such as Articulate, a full component content-management system, or a web CMS. It compares Createspace with Canvas Commons, Frost, Articulate, Adobe Experience Manager and Modern Campus. Those comparisons are internal and must be rechecked before use.

## Open source: customer-scoped evidence

The [Fanshawe open-source LMS differentiation record](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/CustomerMarketingHub/Shared%20Documents/Champions/Content%20Summaries/The%20Grow%20Up/Tara%20Urbach%20-%20Fanshawe%20College%20-%20Competitive%20differentiation%20-%20D2L%20vs%20open%20source%20LMS.pdf?web=1), updated 2026-06-23, records one customer's experience with branding inconsistency, fragmented security/support responsibilities and dedicated-development burden. It is not a universal claim about open-source platforms.

## Governed named-competitor guidance

[[LLM-WIKI D2L/06-Competitors 1/Competitive Cards]] separates three evidence lanes:

1. current approved D2L guidance;
2. historical intelligence that can only inform questions; and
3. automated signals from official vendor pages that represent vendor self-positioning, not comparative proof.

Current dedicated guidance was found for Canvas, Moodle and Blackboard. Current financial-services positioning was found for Absorb, Docebo and Cornerstone, but no current dedicated battlecards were found for those three. [[LLM-WIKI D2L/06-Competitors 1/Competitive Card - Status Quo]] is grounded in current discovery, migration and change-qualification guidance and does not assert a universal cost of inaction.

## Remaining boundaries

The vault still does not contain current approved, general-purpose guidance for build-versus-buy or point-solution-versus-unified-platform decisions. Named-competitor pricing, packaging, roadmap, implementation timelines, legal terms and unvalidated feature gaps remain outside the verified scope. Recheck any comparative fact that could vary by edition, package, region or deployment.
