## Seller decision sequence

1. Start with the buyer workflow and audience.
2. Identify the likely system of record, data flow and operational owner.
3. Check product status, segment, hosting region, language and dependency.
4. Confirm whether the capability is core, add-on, service, limited availability or custom scoped.
5. Use the exact current source before demo, proposal or commitment.

## Claims to avoid

- “Available everywhere” without checking the named product.
- “Included with Brightspace” when the source identifies an add-on or service.
- “Integrates with” as a promise about every object, direction and workflow.
- General-availability language for limited, controlled, beta or in-development capabilities.
- Product outcome claims that are not supported by customer-specific proof.
