# Proof Card - Canadian Sport Institute Calgary - Flexible Coach Education Across Distances

## Context

Canadian Sport Institute (CSI) Calgary built a flexible, user-friendly learning platform on Brightspace Core with Partner Integration to make advanced coaching courses accessible to elite coaches across Canada despite demanding professional schedules.

## Buyer problem

A small, specialized professional association needs to train busy working professionals across a large geography without high travel costs, while meeting data-residency and security expectations.

## What changed

CSI built streaming-video-based courses that let instructors teach remotely, letting coaches learn at their own pace and collaborate across institutions.

## Reported outcome

- Cut travel costs by enabling instructors to teach via streaming video links.
- Reduced attrition by letting students learn at their own pace, anywhere.
- Simplified collaboration with other institutions on innovative online course development.
- Improved security by storing content in a single Canadian data center repository.

## Safe claim

CSI Calgary reports that its Brightspace-based platform cut travel costs and reduced attrition for its 70 coach-learners across Canada by supporting self-paced, remote learning, while storing content in a single Canadian data center for security.

## Do not overstate

- Do not extrapolate cost savings figures — none were quantified beyond "cuts travel costs."
- Do not imply Canadian data residency is guaranteed for all D2L deployments without confirming current hosting options.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Smaller professional/sports associations training working professionals across a wide geography
- Buyers with Canadian data-residency or security requirements
- Associations wanting to collaborate on course development across partner institutions

## Discovery questions

- How much does travel currently cost your organization to deliver in-person training to a dispersed group?
- Do you have specific data-residency or security requirements from your board or regulators?
- Are you interested in co-developing courses with partner institutions or organizations?
