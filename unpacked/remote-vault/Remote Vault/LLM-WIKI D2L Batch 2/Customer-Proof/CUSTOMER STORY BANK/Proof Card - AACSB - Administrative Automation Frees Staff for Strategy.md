# Proof Card - AACSB - Administrative Automation Frees Staff for Strategy

## Context

The Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) used Brightspace Core to automate administrative processes, then used the freed-up capacity to launch a new competency-based education (CBE) academy for its member business schools.

## Buyer problem

Association staff are consumed by time-intensive manual administrative processes, leaving little capacity to build new member-facing programs or revenue lines.

## What changed

AACSB automated process-heavy administrative work and used course templates to speed up content creation, which freed staff to plan and launch a new CBE academy for members.

## Reported outcome

- Automation and process enhancement saved hours of administrative work.
- Course templates accelerated creation of interactive, engaging course content.
- A 50-page process was reduced to a few clicks.
- The competency-based academy now supports professional development for business school staff industry-wide.

## Safe claim

AACSB reports that automating administrative processes on Brightspace — reducing a 50-page process to a few clicks — freed staff capacity to launch a new competency-based education academy for its member business schools.

## Do not overstate

- Do not cite a specific hours-saved figure — none was published beyond "hours."
- Do not imply automation alone caused the new academy launch; it enabled staff capacity, not the strategic decision itself.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Associations where "administration effort" is the primary buying objection
- Organizations wanting to launch a new revenue-generating or member-value program but lack staff bandwidth
- Education-sector professional associations
