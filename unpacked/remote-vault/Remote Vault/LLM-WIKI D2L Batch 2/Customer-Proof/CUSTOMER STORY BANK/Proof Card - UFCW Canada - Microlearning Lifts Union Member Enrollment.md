# Proof Card - UFCW Canada - Microlearning Lifts Union Member Enrollment

## Context

UFCW Canada, a union serving roughly 10,000 learners, built "On-the-Go" (OTG) microlearning modules on Brightspace Core (webCampus) with Optimization Services to fit training into members' demanding, non-desk work schedules.

## Buyer problem

Shift-based or frontline union members can't commit to long, traditional online courses, and content teams need to produce new training faster than a traditional course-build cycle allows.

## What changed

UFCW Canada introduced 15-minute, skill-focused OTG modules without formal testing, designed to fit into busy schedules and address specific workplace challenges directly.

## Reported outcome

- Boosted overall webCampus enrollment by 25% and OTG module enrollment by 55%.
- Cut module creation/deployment time to just a few days, versus months for traditional online courses.
- Lifted OTG completion rates to 57%, well above the 35-40% completion rate of traditional courses.

## Safe claim

UFCW Canada reports that its Brightspace-based microlearning modules lifted overall enrollment 25% and module-specific enrollment 55%, cut content production time from months to days, and achieved 57% completion versus 35-40% for traditional courses.

## Do not overstate

- Do not apply these percentages to non-microlearning course formats.
- Do not promise the same content-production speed without confirming the customer's authoring workflow and team size.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Unions and associations serving shift-based, frontline, or non-desk members
- Organizations wanting to prove microlearning outperforms traditional course completion
- Content teams needing to accelerate authoring velocity

## Discovery questions

- What percentage of your membership works shifts or non-desk jobs that limit access to long training sessions?
- How long does it currently take your team to build and deploy a new course or module?
- What's your current completion rate for traditional (non-micro) courses?
