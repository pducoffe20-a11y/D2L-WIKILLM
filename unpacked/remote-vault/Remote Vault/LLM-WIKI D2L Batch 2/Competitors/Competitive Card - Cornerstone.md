# Competitive Card - Cornerstone

## Governance

- **Current approved guidance:** the [July 2026 Financial Services ICP PMM Playbook](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Corporate/Financial%20Services%20PMM%20Playbook.pdf?web=1) positions Cornerstone around workforce transformation, talent management and compliance for large enterprises. It suggests differentiating through learning experience and support for workforce readiness, onboarding and continuous skills development. This is financial-services scoped.
- **Historical intelligence:** the 2021 feature evaluation and 2022 Xplor overview in the historical CI inventory are not current guidance.
- **Automated competitive signal:** [Cornerstone's official platform page](https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/platform/), captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30, positions Cornerstone Workforce AI around workforce readiness, skills intelligence, learning, talent and enterprise governance.
- **Missing evidence:** no current dedicated, cross-industry D2L-vs-Cornerstone battlecard was found.

## Where they fit

Cornerstone may fit large enterprises seeking learning within a wider talent, skills and workforce-readiness strategy, particularly where suite consolidation and HR ownership are major decision criteria.

## Likely buyer rationale

- A broader talent or workforce platform appears simpler to govern.
- HR wants skills, learning and talent data in one operating model.
- Enterprise compliance, governance and global scale are central.
- The buyer values vendor consolidation over specialist depth.

## Discovery questions

- Is the decision primarily about learning outcomes, talent operations, skills intelligence or suite consolidation?
- Which system owns skills, roles, employee data and compliance records?
- Where does the current suite experience create efficiency, and where does it constrain learning teams?
- What learner, manager and content-author workflows must be demonstrated?
- How are external audiences, continuing education or customer education handled?
- What integrations remain even after choosing a suite?

## Safe differentiation

- Separate suite breadth from the quality and depth of required learning workflows.
- In financial services, explore onboarding, continuous skills development, regulated learning and workforce-readiness evidence.
- Demonstrate Brightspace against the roles and programs that carry the business case.
- Treat integration with the HR/talent ecosystem as an architecture question, not a reason to dismiss the suite.

## Claims to avoid

- Cornerstone learning is merely an HR module or cannot deliver engaging learning.
- A unified suite automatically creates weak learning outcomes.
- D2L has greater scale, governance, AI or skills capability without scoped proof.
- Suite replacement, integration effort, pricing or total cost assumptions.
- Using 2021–2022 Xplor or feature intelligence as current.

## Desired next step

Map the systems of record and select one learning-critical workflow plus one suite-governance workflow for an evidence-based evaluation.
