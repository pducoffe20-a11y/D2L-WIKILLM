# Proof Card - Colliers Project Leaders - Training at Business Scale

## Context

Colliers Project Leaders was growing at an average annual rate of 15%, onboarding up to 10 hires a week, and supporting more than 800 staff with a single training manager.

## Buyer problem

Scale onboarding and continuing professional development without allowing training capacity to constrain company growth.

## What changed

Colliers shifted to a blended, self-paced model. It used Brightspace for course delivery, learner tracking, self-enrollment, automation, reporting, and analytics.

## Reported outcome

- One trainer supported learning for more than 800 staff.
- Course enrollments grew from 4,000 in the first year to more than 10,000, described as 173% growth over three years.
- The 2023 program produced 5,266 course completions.
- 85% of learners rated training and delivery four out of five or higher.

## Safe claim

Colliers Project Leaders reports that a single trainer used Brightspace to support more than 800 staff and scale course enrollments from 4,000 in the first year to more than 10,000.

## Do not overstate

- Do not say Brightspace caused the company’s 15% annual growth; training supported a business already growing at that rate.
- Do not assume one administrator can support the same scale in a different content, governance, or service model.
- Do not present enrollment growth as revenue or verified skills improvement.

## Best fit

- Corporate learning and professional services
- Rapid onboarding
- Lean learning teams
- Buyers linking workforce readiness to business expansion

## Discovery questions

- How many new hires and active learners must the learning team support each month?
- Which activities still depend on a trainer’s direct intervention?
- Where could self-enrollment, notifications, or progress tracking remove workload?
- How do you measure readiness beyond course enrollment and completion?
