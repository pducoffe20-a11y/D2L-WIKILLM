# Value Model - Global Scale

## Use when

The buyer wants to add geographies, languages, business units, partners, customers, members or other external audiences.

## Required inputs

- Baseline and target active learners by audience and geography.
- Paid versus non-paid populations.
- Content localization, translation and accessibility cost.
- Regional support, implementation and administration effort.
- Identity, integration, data residency, privacy and regulatory requirements.
- Variable delivery cost and incremental contribution where applicable.
- Duplicated local-platform or process cost that can actually be retired.

## Formulas

```text
Incremental reach =
  target active learners - baseline active learners

Incremental contribution =
  incremental paid learners
  × buyer-validated unit contribution
  - localization, support and delivery cost

Avoided duplication value =
  retired local platform/process cost
  - new global operating cost
```

Report reach separately from contribution.

## Discovery

- Which audience or geography is first, and why?
- Is the goal reach, workforce readiness, member value, customer education or revenue?
- What must be localized beyond language?
- Which regional systems, policies and support models remain?
- What defines an active learner and a successful program?

## Proof

[[Proof Card - Colliers Project Leaders - Training at Business Scale]] reports one trainer supporting more than 800 staff and enrollment growth from 4,000 to more than 10,000. This shows one operating model at scale, not a staffing ratio or global-cost benchmark.

## Claims to avoid

- Learner growth equals business value.
- Translation alone makes a program locally appropriate or compliant.
- One global instance removes every local tool or process.
- The same support or administrator ratio applies across audiences.
