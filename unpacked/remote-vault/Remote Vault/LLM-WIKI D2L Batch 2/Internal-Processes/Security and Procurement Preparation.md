# Security and Procurement Preparation

## Approved positioning themes

Current approved collateral describes:

- privacy by design across development and operations;
- a security and privacy program aligned to global standards;
- layered technical and operational safeguards;
- access controls and data separation;
- redundancy, scalability, disaster recovery and replicated data supporting continuity.

Use the current collateral for these themes. Use the formal response owner and current evidence package for certifications, architecture, audit artifacts or contractual language.

## Prepare before the review

- Buyer security, privacy, legal and procurement contacts.
- Required questionnaire or control framework.
- Data types, learner populations and sensitivity.
- Hosting and data-residency requirements.
- Authentication and identity design.
- Integration systems and data flows.
- Accessibility, retention, continuity and recovery requirements.
- Required evidence, deadlines and decision dependencies.

## Boundaries

- Do not infer a certification, legal conclusion, data location or contractual commitment.
- Do not answer a security questionnaire from memory.
- Product-level hosting and language availability must be checked separately.
- SSO, local authentication and integration design are implementation inputs, not generic guarantees.
- Restricted security material should remain in its approved system; store only the route and safe summary in the vault.

## Seller next step

Agree on the evidence list, owner, due date and technical-validation meeting before treating security as “complete.”
