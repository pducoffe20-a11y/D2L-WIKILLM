# Proof Card - Singapore Polytechnic - Institution-Wide AI Upskilling

## Context

Singapore Polytechnic needed to establish baseline generative-AI knowledge across more than 1,400 teaching and administrative staff with widely different starting proficiency.

## Buyer problem

Launch consistent, trackable professional learning at institution scale without relying on dozens of face-to-face workshops or forcing every learner through identical content.

## What changed

The institution developed a self-paced Brightspace program in three months. It used self-assessment, adaptive pathways, quizzes, Intelligent Agents, SCORM content, and badges.

## Reported outcome

- More than 1,400 staff were upskilled without the need for 40 in-person workshops.
- As of December 2024, 94% of staff had completed the advanced course.
- 38% of staff advanced directly to advanced content, reducing unnecessary training time.
- Survey results reported 85% agreement that outcomes were clear and relevant, 83% that the package was well organized, and 86% that coverage was adequate.

## Safe claim

Singapore Polytechnic reports that it developed and launched a self-paced Gen AI program in three months, reached more than 1,400 staff, and recorded 94% completion of advanced training by December 2024.

## Do not overstate

- Do not promise a 94% completion rate or three-month development schedule.
- Do not claim the LMS alone produced staff confidence, productivity, or grant awards.
- Do not collapse survey agreement, course completion, and demonstrated competency into the same outcome.
- Do not describe self-assessed routing as an independently validated skills assessment.

## Best fit

- Higher education and K–12 professional learning
- Enterprise AI literacy or emerging-skill programs
- Mixed-proficiency audiences
- Buyers replacing workshop-heavy training

## Discovery questions

- How varied is current proficiency across the target audience?
- Which learners should be able to test or route past foundational material?
- What completion, proficiency, and application measures need to remain distinct?
- What content-development capacity is available before the target launch?
