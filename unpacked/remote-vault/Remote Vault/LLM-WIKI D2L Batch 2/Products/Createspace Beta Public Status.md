# Createspace Beta Public Status

The public page describes Createspace as an environment for creating, organizing, reusing and updating learning content.

## Beta workflow themes

- Navigation, search and folder management across Createspace and Brightspace.
- Content reuse, versioning and collaborative updates.
- Role-based access for administrators, instructional designers and course builders.
- Source-course development and deployment of updates.
- Feedback through surveys, support and product-manager sessions.

## Explicit status boundary

The page identifies Createspace as a beta program and says participants should expect bugs or limitations. It describes the beta as running through July 2026 and says a transition to general availability is planned later in 2026.

## Seller rule

Do not present beta workflows, timing or future general availability as a contracted commitment. Check the current public page and internal product-status source before use.
