## D2L routing contract

1. Search the D2L Sales Brain first and open the strongest notes and cited sources.
2. Derive a narrow public-research topic from the material vault findings, gaps, stale claims, or contradictions.
3. Invoke the vendored `last30days` skill and follow its complete upstream `SKILL.md` contract. Do not imitate or partially reimplement it.
4. Treat the returned material as public research:
   - first-party statements and authoritative public records may establish public facts within their scope;
   - vendor claims remain vendor claims;
   - social, community, engagement, and momentum data remain signals;
   - none of these change a vault note's status or verification scope.
5. Reconcile the public findings with the vault as `corroborates`, `updates`, `contradicts`, `contextualizes`, or `no evidence found`.
6. Keep the upstream `last30days` output intact when the user invokes it directly. In a D2L seller brief, add a separate reconciliation after that output rather than rewriting its required output shape.
7. Do not auto-capture the research. Propose a dry-run capture to `90-Inbox` if it has durable value.
8. Credentials, browser-cookie access, optional setup, publishing, and external writes remain subject to the upstream skill's consent gates and the D2L authorization boundary.
