# Value Model - Time to Launch

## Use when

There is a defined launch date, delayed benefit, event, regulatory requirement or market window.

## Required inputs

- Current expected launch date and basis.
- Validated proposed launch date for an equivalent scope.
- Minimum viable scope versus full future-state scope.
- Monthly contribution or avoided cost available only after launch.
- Cost of acceleration, parallel work and services.
- Probability of meeting each schedule.
- Value lost if the compelling event is missed.

## Formula

```text
Expected months accelerated =
  (baseline launch date - proposed launch date)
  × schedule-confidence factor

Earlier contribution =
  expected months accelerated × validated monthly contribution

Net launch value =
  earlier contribution + buyer-validated avoided delay cost
  - acceleration cost
```

Do not add the same earlier revenue in both this model and [[Value Model - Revenue Growth]].

## Discovery

- What must be available by the date?
- What can be phased after launch?
- Which content, integration, security and acceptance dependencies control the schedule?
- Who can make scope decisions quickly?
- Is the value from earlier revenue, continuity, avoided event cancellation or strategic readiness?

## Proof

[[Proof Card - CHIME - Accelerated Digital Learning Launch]] reports a two-week initial launch after narrowing a planned nine-month scope and augmenting the team with D2L services. It is not a two-week full-implementation benchmark.

## Claims to avoid

- D2L can launch every buyer in two weeks.
- Content creation speed equals end-to-end implementation speed.
- An earlier date has value without a defined benefit or avoided consequence.
- Schedule confidence is 100%.
