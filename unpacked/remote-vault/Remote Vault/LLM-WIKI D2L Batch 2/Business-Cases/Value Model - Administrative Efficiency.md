# Value Model - Administrative Efficiency

## Use when

A repeated workflow has measurable volume, handling time and labor ownership.

## Required inputs

For each workflow:

- Annual transaction volume.
- Current average handling time.
- Expected future handling time from a pilot or validated design.
- Fully loaded hourly labor cost approved by finance.
- Adoption and realization factor.
- Error, rework or escalation volume where material.
- New administration or support effort introduced by the solution.

## Formula

```text
Annual gross hours saved =
  annual volume × (current minutes - future minutes) / 60

Realized capacity hours =
  gross hours saved × realization factor

Equivalent capacity value =
  realized capacity hours × loaded hourly cost

Net capacity value =
  equivalent capacity value - incremental operating cost
```

Report cashable savings only when an approved budget, contractor cost, overtime expense or planned hire will actually be reduced or avoided.

## Workflow candidates

- Enrollment and user administration.
- Course creation, copying and updating.
- Quiz, rubric, caption or feedback preparation.
- Reporting and data reconciliation.
- Certification and renewal tracking.
- Support triage and common questions.
- Evaluation and grading administration.

## Proof

- [[Proof Card - Year Up United - Consolidation and Administrative Efficiency]] reports 41 hours eliminated per 1,000 enrollments and up to 16 hours per evaluation cycle.
- [[Proof Card - Epworth HealthCare - Compliance Learning Efficiency]] reports content delivered five times faster and development costs reduced 30%.
- The current D2L Lumi pitchbook contains a 60–80% webinar course-building time signal and 93% chat-deflection signal explicitly marked internal only. Do not use either as a default input or external claim.

## Claims to avoid

- Hours saved are cash savings.
- Automation eliminates a role or approved headcount.
- A customer or internal metric predicts the buyer's workflow.
- Faster creation preserves quality without validation.

## Validation

Time a representative sample before and after the proposed workflow. Have the process owner validate volume and finance validate labor treatment.
