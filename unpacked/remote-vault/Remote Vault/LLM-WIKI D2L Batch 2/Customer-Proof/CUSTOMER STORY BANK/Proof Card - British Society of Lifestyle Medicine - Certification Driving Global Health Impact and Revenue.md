# Proof Card - British Society of Lifestyle Medicine - Certification Driving Global Health Impact and Revenue

## Context

The British Society of Lifestyle Medicine (BSLM) partnered with D2L, using Brightspace Core, Creator+, and Course Merchant to build flexible certification programs for healthcare professionals, addressing an educational gap in lifestyle medicine in the UK and overseas.

## Buyer problem

A healthcare-adjacent association needs to scale certification globally, grow membership and non-dues revenue, and prove measurable downstream impact (patient care) to justify the investment.

## What changed

BSLM built scalable certification programs on Brightspace with a revenue-share model and global partnerships, monetizing course delivery directly through Course Merchant.

## Reported outcome

- Influenced care for over 3 million patients annually (member-reported downstream impact).
- Delivered 60,000+ hours of accredited lifestyle medicine learning globally.
- 150% membership increase over recent years, with a continued 20% annual growth rate since 2021.
- 335% increase in annual conference attendance.
- 46% increase in total income, followed by a further 120%+ rise the next year.
- New revenue unlocked through a scalable revenue-share model and global partnerships.

## Safe claim

BSLM reports that its Brightspace-based certification program grew membership 150% and total income 46% (with a further 120%+ rise the following year), while scaling to 60,000+ hours of accredited learning delivered globally through a revenue-share and partnership model.

## Do not overstate

- Do not present "3 million patients" as a Brightspace-caused outcome — it is the association's self-reported downstream estimate of certified professionals' reach, not a platform metric.
- Do not imply the income growth is solely attributable to the LMS; the revenue-share model and partnerships are explicitly cited as contributing factors.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Healthcare and clinical-adjacent professional associations
- Associations building or scaling a non-dues revenue / certification-as-a-business model
- Buyers evaluating Course Merchant for direct monetization of courses

## Discovery questions

- What percentage of your association's revenue currently comes from certification or course sales versus membership dues?
- Are you currently able to sell courses directly to non-members, and if so, how?
- What would a meaningful increase in annual conference attendance or membership mean for your organization's sustainability?
