# Commercial Learning Executive

## Seller truth

An executive or director-level buyer whose mandate includes expanding course reach and monetizing learning as a revenue line (non-dues revenue for associations, or new program revenue for other segments). The Course Merchant Overview associates executives with reach expansion and directors with monetization; it does not name a universal title, so confirm the actual role in discovery.

## Why it matters

Persona notes guide discovery without inventing priorities. This persona is the economic-buyer analog for Course Merchant-led conversations, distinct from the operational Learning Operations Leader persona.

## When to use it

Discovery and proposal conversations where course monetization, non-dues revenue, or paid external course delivery is the primary motivation — most commonly in association and training-organization segments.

## Evidence

Course Merchant Overview (source_url above) associates executive-level buyers with expanding course reach, and director-level buyers with monetizing learning. This is source-bounded seller guidance, not a universal title mapping — do not assume this role exists or holds budget authority at every account.

## Seller interpretation

**Interpretation:** This persona is likely present when the deal motivation includes external course sales, non-dues revenue, or a new revenue-generating academy — see [[Proof Card - British Society of Lifestyle Medicine - Certification Driving Global Health Impact and Revenue]] for a customer example of this buyer's success criteria in practice.

## Talk track

I'd like to understand how course delivery connects to revenue goals for your organization — what does success look like from a growth or non-dues revenue perspective?

## Discovery questions

- What percentage of your revenue today comes from course or certification sales versus other sources?
- Who signs off on a new revenue-generating program, and what proof do they need to say yes?
- Are you selling to members only, or also to a broader external audience?
- What's the current path from "course built" to "course sold" — how many manual steps are involved?

## Objections and cautions

Titles do not prove authority, budget, or ownership. The Course Merchant Overview does not name a single universal title for this persona — confirm the actual title, reporting line, and budget authority during discovery rather than assuming.
