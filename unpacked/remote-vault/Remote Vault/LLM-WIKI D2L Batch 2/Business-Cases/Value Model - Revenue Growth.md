# Value Model - Revenue Growth

## Use when

The buyer sells courses, programs, subscriptions or credentials and can supply demand, price, volume and delivery-cost data.

## Required inputs

| Input | Definition |
|---|---|
| Baseline paid enrollments | Completed paid registrations in the baseline period |
| Target paid enrollments | Buyer-approved scenario, not a D2L promise |
| Net realized price | Collected revenue after discounts, refunds and taxes excluded from revenue |
| Variable delivery cost | Payment fees, royalties, instructor or fulfillment cost that changes with volume |
| Incremental program cost | Marketing, content, staffing and support added to pursue growth |
| Realization factor | Portion of forecast expected after adoption and launch ramp |

## Formula

```text
Unit contribution = net realized price - variable delivery cost

Incremental contribution =
  (target paid enrollments - baseline paid enrollments)
  × unit contribution
  × realization factor
  - incremental program cost
```

Model new audiences, price changes and renewal separately. Do not add gross revenue and contribution as two benefits.

## Discovery

- Which offerings already have demonstrated demand?
- What constrains growth: awareness, capacity, registration, content, geography or delivery?
- What percentage of registrations are discounted, refunded or unpaid?
- Which costs rise with each learner?
- Is the objective gross revenue, contribution, reach or strategic member value?

## Proof

[[Proof Card - Good Roads - Scalable Association Training]] reports tenfold online-enrollment growth in two years and a self-sustaining program. It does not publish price, revenue, margin or payback and therefore supplies no unit-value input.

## Claims to avoid

- Course Merchant or Brightspace will create demand.
- Another buyer will achieve tenfold enrollment growth.
- Gross revenue is equivalent to profit or value.
- Faster content creation automatically creates revenue.

## Validation

Finance owns unit contribution. Marketing or program leadership owns demand assumptions. Reforecast after the first paid cohort using actual traffic, conversion, refunds and delivery cost.
