# Change Management Consulting Positioning

Change Management Consulting is an add-on service focused on the people side of transformation. It complements project management and embedded change support rather than replacing implementation.

## Fit signals

- Low or inconsistent adoption.
- Limited internal change-management capacity.
- Complex stakeholder groups.
- Change resistance or fatigue.
- A defined launch target with an unclear adoption path.
- Need for leadership alignment, champion networks or structured communications.

## Deliverable themes

- Change-readiness and needs assessment.
- Leadership and stakeholder alignment.
- Communication strategy and key messaging.
- Champion-network and enablement planning.
- Integrated change timeline and risk analysis.
- Adoption and usage insights.
- Sustainment recommendations and an optimization roadmap.

## Sales process boundary

Every engagement is custom scoped. The current source requires internal alignment and a scoping conversation before deliverables, hours or commercial terms are defined. Do not quote sample hours, rates, timelines or outcomes from this note.

## Evidence boundary

Industry change-management benchmarks cited in the source are not D2L customer outcomes. They must not be presented as guaranteed results of the service.

## Discovery questions

- What behaviors must change for the implementation to create value?
- Which stakeholder groups are most affected?
- Who is the visible executive sponsor?
- What resistance, capacity or communication risks already exist?
- How will adoption be measured after launch?
- What internal change capability is available today?
