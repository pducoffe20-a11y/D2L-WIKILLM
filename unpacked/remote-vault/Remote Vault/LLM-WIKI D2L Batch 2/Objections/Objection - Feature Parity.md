# Objection - Feature Parity


    ## Seller truth

    Distinguish required outcomes from inherited feature checklists.

    ## Why it matters

    An objection response should reduce uncertainty and advance discovery.

    ## When to use it

    Live buyer conversation, follow-up, or proposal review.

    ## Evidence

    Batch 1 evidence supports this as a seller discovery and qualification pattern for associations. It does not prove that the condition exists for a specific buyer. Use the association pitch deck, healthcare-association collateral, Product Resources hub, Course Merchant overview, and Corporate Use Cases page according to their authority and freshness.

    ## Seller interpretation

    **Interpretation:** The objection may be a request for evidence, process clarity, or risk ownership.

    ## Talk track

    That’s a fair concern. Could we define what would make this risk acceptable for your team?

    ## Discovery questions

    - What specifically worries you?
- What evidence would help?
- Who needs to be comfortable with the answer?

    ## Objections and cautions

    **Behind it (hypothesis):** a checklist comparison is masking a priority or migration concern.
    **Diagnose:** Which capability is decision-critical, and what outcome depends on it?
    **Use:** current product documentation and a bounded test; do not speculate about competitors.
    **Next step:** convert the checklist into agreed decision criteria.
