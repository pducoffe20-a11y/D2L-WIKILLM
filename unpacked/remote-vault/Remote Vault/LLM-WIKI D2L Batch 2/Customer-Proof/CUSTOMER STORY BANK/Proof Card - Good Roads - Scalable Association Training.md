# Proof Card - Good Roads - Scalable Association Training

## Context

Good Roads is a municipal association serving infrastructure professionals, including learners in rural and remote communities. It moved beyond a small online pilot to a broader continuing-education offer using Brightspace Core, H5P, and D2L Learning Services.

## Buyer problem

Expand access to training without depending only on in-person delivery, while maintaining course quality and building a financially sustainable education program.

## What changed

Good Roads launched nine online courses in two years and expanded from a 40-learner pilot to hundreds of learners.

## Reported outcome

- Online enrollment grew 10x in two years.
- Course completion exceeded 85%.
- More than 80% of learners scored above 80% on assessments.
- 85% of learners said they were likely to take another course.
- The customer reports that the online courses became self-sustaining and net-positive.

## Safe claim

Good Roads reports that its Brightspace-based program expanded online enrollment tenfold in two years while maintaining completion above 85% and becoming self-sustaining.

## Do not overstate

- Do not promise 10x growth, profitability, or a specific completion rate for another organization.
- Do not omit the customer’s course strategy, market demand, funding context, content work, or Learning Services support.
- Do not translate “net-positive” into a margin, revenue, or payback figure not published in the story.

## Best fit

- Associations and credentialing bodies
- Continuing education and non-dues revenue
- Rural or geographically distributed learners
- Buyers testing whether online education can become a sustainable line of business

## Discovery questions

- Which audiences cannot reliably access your current in-person programs?
- What limits enrollment today: capacity, geography, scheduling, or course production?
- Which existing programs have enough demand to support an online pilot?
- How would you define a sustainable program: reach, completion, renewal, revenue, or contribution margin?
