# Persona-Outcome Cross-Reference Matrix

## Overview

This matrix maps the four key association buyer personas to the five core buyer outcomes. Use it to:
- **Tailor discovery** to each stakeholder's priorities
- **Prepare stakeholder-specific messaging** before meetings
- **Build buying committees** by identifying shared and competing priorities
- **Plan account strategy** around outcome ownership and approval gates

## Outcome priority matrix

| Outcome | VP Education | CFO | CIO | eLearning Mgr | Board / CEO |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Non-Dues Revenue** | Primary | Primary | Secondary | Nice-to-have | Primary |
| **Member Retention / NPS** | Primary | Important | Not Primary | Primary | Primary |
| **Authoring Efficiency** | Important | Important | Secondary | Primary | Not Primary |
| **Systems Integration** | Secondary | Secondary | Primary | Important | Not Primary |
| **Security & Compliance** | Important | Primary | Primary | Secondary | Primary |

## Outcome detail & messaging by persona

### 1. Non-Dues Revenue

**Why it matters:**
Non-dues revenue drives organizational sustainability and member value. Growth in this metric justifies further investment in education and credentialing programs.

**VP Education (Primary):**
- Primary driver of strategic success; central to role evaluation
- Focus: Course completion rate (+28%), member registration growth (2.7x), revenue impact
- Messaging: "D2L's personalized learning paths and mobile experience drive 28% higher completion rates—directly increasing non-dues revenue per program."

**CFO (Primary):**
- Direct P&L impact; owns business case economics
- Focus: Payback period, gross/net revenue from CE, cost per active learner
- Messaging: "Reduce course development time by 30% while increasing completion rates by 28%—your non-dues revenue grows while costs drop."

**CIO (Secondary):**
- Indirect impact through system reliability and integration efficiency
- Focus: System uptime enabling member access; integration reliability supporting revenue operations
- Messaging: "99.99% uptime SLA means your revenue-generating programs never go down. Automated AMS integrations mean no revenue leakage from enrollment errors."

**eLearning Manager (Nice-to-have):**
- Indirect; depends on VP Education and CFO to communicate value
- Focus: Enabling higher completion through better course design
- Messaging: "Creator+ templates let you design courses that keep learners engaged—higher engagement = higher completion."

**Board / CEO (Primary):**
- Strategic imperative for organizational growth and independence from dues
- Focus: Revenue growth, market competitiveness, member satisfaction
- Messaging: "Education programs are your path to revenue diversification. D2L enables higher completion and faster course launch—essential for market leadership."

### 2. Member Retention / NPS

**Why it matters:**
Member retention is the foundation of organizational stability. Engaged members renew memberships and recommend programs to peers.

**VP Education (Primary):**
- Core accountability; linked to overall role success
- Focus: Course engagement, NPS improvement, time-to-credential reduction
- Messaging: "Members who complete CE programs renew at 2x+ the rate of non-participants. D2L's engagement tools and community integration keep your members coming back."

**CFO (Important):**
- Indirect impact through lifetime value and churn reduction
- Focus: Revenue predictability from renewal rates
- Messaging: "Retention rate improvements directly reduce acquisition costs and increase lifetime value—key metrics for finance board reporting."

**CIO (Not Primary):**
- Minimal direct ownership; supports infrastructure for engagement
- Focus: System performance supporting engagement metrics
- Messaging: "System reliability enables consistent member experience; poor uptime drives member frustration and churn."

**eLearning Manager (Primary):**
- Direct impact through course design, engagement, accessibility
- Focus: Learning experience quality, completion satisfaction, accessibility compliance
- Messaging: "Members who can access and complete your courses—especially on mobile, with WCAG AA compliance—report higher satisfaction and stay longer."

**Board / CEO (Primary):**
- Strategic metric; linked to organizational survival and growth
- Focus: Member lifetime value, competitive differentiation
- Messaging: "Member retention is existential. Education programs drive loyalty. Invest in programs that keep members engaged."

### 3. Authoring Efficiency

**Why it matters:**
Course development speed determines how quickly you can respond to market changes, member requests, and regulatory updates. Efficiency directly impacts team capacity and costs.

**VP Education (Important):**
- Operational concern affecting team capacity and launch velocity
- Focus: Time-to-revenue for new programs, team bandwidth
- Messaging: "30% faster course development means your team ships new programs 4+ weeks sooner. That's competitive speed."

**CFO (Important):**
- Cost impact; reduces headcount needs and external authoring costs
- Focus: Cost per course, elimination of third-party authoring licenses
- Messaging: "30% faster course development + built-in templates eliminate expensive third-party authoring tools. Direct margin improvement."

**CIO (Secondary):**
- Technical concern around tools integration and infrastructure
- Focus: Integration with content management, performance
- Messaging: "Brightspace Creator+ integrates natively with your AMS; no custom middleware or API development needed."

**eLearning Manager (Primary):**
- Central to daily work; primary influencer on tool selection
- Focus: Tool usability, template quality, learning curve, import capabilities
- Messaging: "Creator+ templates let you build interactive courses 3+ weeks faster than traditional authoring. Your team ships more with the same headcount."

**Board / CEO (Not Primary):**
- Indirect; valuable only if communicated by operational leadership
- Focus: Team productivity and competitive speed-to-market
- Messaging: "Operational efficiency in course development enables faster innovation and competitive response."

### 4. Systems Integration

**Why it matters:**
Integration with existing AMS/CRM systems is table-stakes. Poor integration creates data silos, manual work, and lost revenue visibility.

**VP Education (Secondary):**
- Operational concern; CIO owns technical details
- Focus: Enrollment accuracy, member data flow, integration reliability
- Messaging: "Seamless AMS integration means no enrollment delays, accurate member data, and no manual workarounds."

**CFO (Secondary):**
- Indirect impact through operational efficiency and compliance
- Focus: Data governance, audit trail for revenue
- Messaging: "Native AMS integration ensures accurate revenue tracking and audit compliance; no manual reconciliation needed."

**CIO (Primary):**
- Central to role; owns integration architecture and compliance
- Focus: API capabilities, SSO/SAML, REST, LTI 1.3, OneRoster support
- Messaging: "D2L's 1EdTech-certified APIs and pre-built AMS connectors eliminate custom integration work. REST APIs enable future custom development."

**eLearning Manager (Important):**
- Indirect; impacts workflow when integrations fail
- Focus: Automation of course provisioning, roster sync
- Messaging: "Automated course provisioning from AMS roster data saves hours per course and eliminates manual enrollment errors."

**Board / CEO (Not Primary):**
- Technical detail owned by CIO
- Focus: Overall system landscape alignment
- Messaging: "Integration with existing systems is essential for operational continuity and data accuracy."

### 5. Security & Compliance

**Why it matters:**
Compliance violations carry legal, financial, and reputational risk. Data breaches expose member data and undermine trust.

**VP Education (Important):**
- Regulatory and member trust concern
- Focus: WCAG accessibility compliance, member data protection, audit readiness
- Messaging: "WCAG 2.1 AA compliance protects you from lawsuits and expands your addressable member base. D2L's templates are accessible by default."

**CFO (Primary):**
- Financial and legal risk management
- Focus: SOC2 Type II, breach risk, regulatory fines, insurance implications
- Messaging: "SOC2 Type II certification and 99.99% uptime SLA reduce insurance costs and eliminate hidden compliance risks. Open-source tools expose you to unknown vulnerabilities."

**CIO (Primary):**
- Central to role; owns security architecture and compliance audit
- Focus: SOC2 Type II, GDPR/FERPA compliance, disaster recovery, incident response
- Messaging: "D2L's AWS managed cloud infrastructure, SOC2 Type II certification, and compliance automation eliminate your security operations burden."

**eLearning Manager (Secondary):**
- Minimal direct ownership; supports compliance through accessible content
- Focus: WCAG compliance in course design
- Messaging: "Creator+ templates ensure WCAG 2.1 AA compliance out of the box; you don't need to audit every course for accessibility."

**Board / CEO (Primary):**
- Organizational risk and reputation management
- Focus: Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, reputation protection
- Messaging: "Regulatory compliance and data security are non-negotiable. D2L's managed cloud and SOC2 certification protect your organization."

## Account planning implications

### High-alignment outcomes
**Non-Dues Revenue + Member Retention:** These are VP Education and CFO priorities. Lead with these outcomes in initial positioning and business case development.

**Security & Compliance:** VP Education, CFO, CIO, and Board share concern here. Use this to break ties and accelerate CIO approval.

### Conflict resolution
**Authoring Efficiency (eLearning Mgr, VP Education) vs. Budget (CFO):** Bridge with TCO model showing savings from reduced third-party licensing and staff productivity gains.

**Systems Integration (CIO) vs. Speed (VP Education):** Position D2L's pre-built AMS connectors as "fast integration, not custom development."

### Buying committee composition
- **Commercial thread:** VP Education + CFO (outcomes: Non-Dues Revenue, Member Retention)
- **Technical thread:** CIO + IT team (outcomes: Systems Integration, Security & Compliance)
- **User acceptance thread:** eLearning Mgr + course design team (outcomes: Authoring Efficiency, Member Retention)
- **Executive approval:** Board/CEO + VP Education (all outcomes, strategic fit)
