## Industry Overview

### Higher Education

**Description**: Universities, colleges, community colleges, and other post-secondary institutions

**Market characteristics**:
- Large institutional deployments
- Mix of on-campus and online learning
- Compliance and accreditation requirements
- Multi-department implementations

**Key customers**: See [[Customer Story Navigator]] and filter by Higher Education

**Typical use cases**:
- Learning Management
- Assessment and Evaluation
- Student Engagement
- Analytics and Outcomes Tracking
- Institutional Effectiveness

**Primary products**:
- [[MOC#Brightspace Learning]] - LMS and engagement
- [[MOC#Brightspace Colleague]] - Student information and integration
- [[MOC#Brightspace Ledger]] - Analytics and outcomes

**Relevant documentation**:
- See [[03-Industries/]] for detailed Higher Ed content
- Check [[Customer Story Navigator]] for customer stories
