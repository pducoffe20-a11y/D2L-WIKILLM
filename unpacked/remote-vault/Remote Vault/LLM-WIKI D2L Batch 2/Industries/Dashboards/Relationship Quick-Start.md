# Relationship Quick-Start: 30 High-Impact Connections

This is your action list. These 30 connections will immediately create insight paths and answer common sales questions. **Start with Priority 1 this week.**

> **Format:** `Source Note` → `Relationship Type` → `Target Note` | **Creates insight:** *what question does this answer?*

---

## Priority 1: Connect All Customer Stories (Do This Week)

Every customer story should link to:
1. The industry context (playbook)
2. The products they used
3. The outcomes they achieved
