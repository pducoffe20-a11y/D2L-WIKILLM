## Start with Needs Framework

When pitching to associations, anchor conversations on what you already know about your target:

1. **If you know their main goal** -> Start with **Business Outcomes**
   - Increase member engagement and retention
   - Boost productivity
   - Generate non-dues revenue
2. **If you only know their role** -> Start with **Personas**
   - Board Member / Executive (Executive Erin)
   - Membership Manager (Membership Manager Mo)
   - Member (Member Melanie)
3. **If you only know a need** -> Start with **Use Cases**
   - High-Impact Learning Experiences
   - Certifications and Credentialing
   - Content Creation & Process Improvement
   - Data-Driven Insights & Monetize Learning
4. **If you want to know a required capability** -> Start with **Products**
   - D2L for Associations Package
   - Creator+
   - Performance+
   - D2L Link or D2L Lumi
   - D2L Course Merchant

## Types of Associations

Associations are segmented into two primary models with distinct member structures and goals:

### 1. Professional Associations
- **Member Structure:** The *individual* is the member.
- **Key Offerings / Goals:**
  - Learning as a member benefit
  - Learning opportunities to increase non-dues revenue
  - Professional training opportunities for non-members

### 2. Trade Associations
- **Member Structure:** The *organization* is the member.
- **Key Offerings / Goals:**
  - Learning to employees of member organizations
  - Training for prospective employees looking to enter that industry

## Business Outcomes

### Increase Member Engagement and Retention
- **Target Personas:** Board Member/Executive (Executive Erin), Membership Manager (Membership Manager Mo)
- **Related Use Cases:** High-Impact Learning Experiences, Certifications and Credentialing
- **Facilitating Product:** D2L for Associations Package

#### Challenge
As member preferences and demographics change, associations need to create additional value to drive initial membership join rates and ongoing renewals.

#### Solution Messaging
**Create Value for Your Members throughout their Membership Journey**
Make learning your competitive advantage by offering digitally-driven, high-impact learning experiences catering to member needs from onboarding to renewal, keeping them on top of industry standards and connected to their professional community.

#### Proof Points / Metrics
- **25% increase in course completion** at (ISC)²

---

### Boost Productivity
- **Target Persona:** Membership Manager (Membership Manager Mo)
- **Related Use Cases:** Content Creation, Process Improvement
- **Facilitating Products:** Creator+, D2L Link and Lumi

#### Challenge
To streamline workflows and save time day-to-day, associations need to improve operational efficiencies and evolve processes through automation and digital transformation.

#### Solution Messaging
**Streamline Workflows**
Get the full picture on education programs from course creation to reporting with an end-to-end solution enhanced with automation, AI, and integrations.

#### Proof Points / Metrics
- **70+ online courses supported by just 3 staff** and over 200 volunteer instructors at **Good Roads**

---

### Generate Non-Dues Revenue
- **Target Persona:** Board Member/Executive (Executive Erin)
- **Related Use Cases:** Data-Driven Insights, Monetize Learning
- **Facilitating Products:** Performance+, D2L Course Merchant

#### Challenge
Associations need the right data at the right time to make decisions that help scale reach and inform new revenue streams for growth.

#### Solution Messaging
**Leverage Learning as Non-Dues Revenue**
Monetize learning programs with e-commerce and unlock additional revenue streams that help achieve growth and stay competitive.

#### Proof Points / Metrics
- **49% uplift in learner engagement and successful commercialization** of training offerings at **Gafta**

## Personas

### Executive Erin (Executive / Board Member)
- **Possible Titles:** CEO, COO, CIO, CTO, Board Member, Chair, President, Vice President, Executive/Membership/Education Director
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Membership Growth and Retention, Staff Productivity and Satisfaction
- **Jobs-To-Be-Done:**
  - Attract new members and retain current members to grow membership base
  - Ensure financial stability and maximize profitability of revenue streams, especially non-dues revenue

#### Key Challenges
1. **Creating More Value for Members:** Improving unique advantages of membership and programs to stay competitive and keep innovating.
2. **Mitigating Financial Risk & ROI:** Accessing strategic insights to measure trends, measure ROI of programs, and make data-driven decisions.
3. **Scaling with Limited Resources:** Evolving processes through digital transformation and equipping staff with expert support and tools.

---

### Membership Manager Mo (Membership / Education Manager)
- **Focus:** Day-to-day operations, course creation, managing volunteer instructors, streamlining workflows, and member onboarding.
- **Related Outcomes:** Boost Productivity, Increase Member Engagement and Retention
- **Related Use Cases:** Content Creation, Process Improvement, High-Impact Learning Experiences

---

### Member Melanie (Association Member / Learner)
- **Focus:** Professional development, earning certifications and credentials, staying on top of industry standards, and connecting with a professional community.
- **Value Drivers:** Seamless learning experience, mobile access, CPD/CE tracking, and high-impact digital learning.
