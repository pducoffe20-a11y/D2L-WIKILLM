# Association Buyer Outcomes Matrix

## Overview

This matrix documents baseline metrics, target outcomes achieved by D2L customers, and the specific D2L capabilities that enable each outcome. Use these for discovery, business case development, and ROI quantification—but always validate with buyer-specific data and circumstances.

## Non-Dues Revenue & Member Growth

| Metric | Baseline | Target Achieved | D2L Enabler |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Course Completion Rate** | 45–55% | +28% increase (ISC2 case study) | Personalized learning paths, automated reminders, mobile UI |
| **Community Session Engagement** | Baseline engagement | 3.4x increase in sessions | Integrated Brightspace & Community tools (Vanilla integration) |
| **Member Registration Growth** | Flat registration | 2.7x growth in membership | Frictionless catalog & e-commerce integration |

### Seller interpretation

Course completion drives non-dues revenue and member satisfaction. A 28% increase in completion represents significant revenue impact; lower completion leaves revenue on the table. Member registration growth reflects increased engagement and program attractiveness. Community integration keeps members engaged beyond formal learning.

## Operational & Authoring Efficiency

| Metric | Baseline | Target Achieved | D2L Enabler |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Course Development Time** | 10–12 weeks / course | 30% reduction (South Univ case study) | Brightspace Creator+ interactive templates |
| **Course Provisioning** | Manual setup (hours/course) | 100% automated provisioning | Automated AMS/CRM rules & API sync |
| **Support Ticket Resolution** | Delayed responses | 66% faster response time | Built-in Brightspace Learning Center & knowledge tools |

### Seller interpretation

Course development speed directly impacts time-to-revenue and team productivity. A 30% reduction in development time compounds across programs: fewer courses take 10 weeks instead of 14.5. Automated provisioning eliminates tedious manual work and reduces errors. Faster support resolution improves member satisfaction and reduces escalations.

## Risk Mitigation & Compliance

| Metric | Baseline | Target Achieved | D2L Enabler |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accessibility Compliance** | Partial / Risk of lawsuit | 100% WCAG 2.1 AA compliant | Native accessible templates & VPAT documentation |
| **Uptime & Security SLA** | 98.5% (Open Source/Self-hosted) | 99.99% availability SLA | SOC2 Type II managed AWS cloud infrastructure |

### Seller interpretation

Accessibility compliance is both a legal requirement and member satisfaction driver. Non-compliance exposes organizations to lawsuits and reduces addressable audience. WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is table-stakes for professional associations. Uptime SLA directly impacts member experience; 99.99% translates to <9 minutes downtime/month vs. 3.6 hours/month at 98.5%.

## Use rules and cautions

- **Validate with buyer context:** Baseline and target metrics are segment-wide patterns, not guarantees. Confirm applicability to specific buyer circumstances, legacy system state, team capability, and member demographics.
- **Use as discovery anchors:** These outcomes guide discovery conversations; they don't replace buyer-specific evidence. Ask "Is the 45–55% completion range relevant to your programs?" before quoting results.
- **Attribution requires evidence:** The presence of a D2L enabler does not prove causality. ISC2 and South Univ achieved these results; your buyer may have different constraints.
- **Include TCO and timeline:** Revenue upside is compelling only with realistic implementation costs, timeline, and payback period. Use the [[Business Case Toolkit]] for governed ROI models.
