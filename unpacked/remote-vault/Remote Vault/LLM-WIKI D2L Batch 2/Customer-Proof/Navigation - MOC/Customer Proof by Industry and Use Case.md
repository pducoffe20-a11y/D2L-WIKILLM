## Safe use

Use the source's own vertical, customer context, problem, named product and reported outcome. Verify the exact source and permission before presenting a quote, metric or reference.

## Selection rule

The sources read do not verify a complete proof catalog across every requested industry and use case. They also do not establish a universal similarity algorithm. Never turn a customer-specific metric into a general product outcome.
