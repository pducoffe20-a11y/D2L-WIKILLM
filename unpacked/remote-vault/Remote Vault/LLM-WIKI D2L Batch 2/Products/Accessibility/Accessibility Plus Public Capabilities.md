# Accessibility Plus Public Capabilities

Accessibility+ is described as a human-led model combining reporting and expert guidance.

## Public workflow

1. Scan content and receive an accessibility report.
2. Review the report with D2L experts and identify priorities.
3. Receive consultative guidance on what to fix and why.
4. Access remediation support where needed.
5. Deliver updated learning content.

## Role-level value described by the page

- Learners: equivalent content access and flexible formats.
- Content creators: proactive suggestions and expert support.
- Leaders and administrators: accessibility data, audit trails and resource prioritization.

## Boundaries

- The page positions Accessibility+ as a supported service model, not an automatic legal-compliance guarantee.
- Legal deadlines and third-party prevalence statistics on the page require independent legal or primary-source verification before reuse.
- Do not treat an accessibility report as proof that remediation has been completed.
