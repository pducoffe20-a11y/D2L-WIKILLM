# Proof Card - National Society of High School Scholars - Youth Employability Nonprofit Program

## Context

The National Society of High School Scholars (NSHSS), a not-for-profit, built the Intern Readiness Certificate Program (IRCP) on Brightspace Core with Course Merchant and Onboarding Services to help students build workplace-ready soft skills.

## Buyer problem

A youth-serving nonprofit needs to help students build leadership and workplace skills before their first job, with visibility for both students and parents, and needs the program to grow enrollment year over year.

## What changed

NSHSS launched IRCP as a unique online course focused on leadership, teamwork, and workplace-readiness skills, with visibility features for parents.

## Reported outcome

- 27% boost in student enrollment year over year.
- Improved communication with students encouraged higher participation.
- Parents gained full visibility into their child's progress.
- Over 1,150 total participants since program inception, 456 in the most recent year on D2L.

## Safe claim

NSHSS reports that its Intern Readiness Certificate Program grew student enrollment 27% year over year on Brightspace, with parent-visibility features supporting engagement across more than 1,150 participants since the program began.

## Do not overstate

- Do not treat the 27% growth figure as guaranteed for a different-sized youth program.
- Do not imply the parent-visibility feature alone drove enrollment growth — communication changes were cited as a contributing factor.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Youth-serving nonprofits and not-for-profits building skills/employability programs
- Programs with a dual audience (student learner + parent stakeholder)
- Organizations monetizing course access via Course Merchant

## Discovery questions

- Does your program need visibility or reporting for a secondary stakeholder (parent, guardian, employer)?
- What's your current year-over-year enrollment trend, and what's driving or limiting it?
- Are you currently able to sell or register learners directly through your platform?
