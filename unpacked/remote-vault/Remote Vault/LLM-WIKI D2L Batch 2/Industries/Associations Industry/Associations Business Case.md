# Increase Member Engagement and Retention

- **Related Use Cases:** [[High-Impact Learning Experiences]], [[Certifications and Credentialing]]
- **Facilitating Product:** [[LLM-WIKI D2L/03-Industries 1/Associations Industry/D4A Package]]

## Challenge
As member expectations shift, associations need to show clear value from the first interaction through renewal. Static education programs and disconnected learning experiences can make it harder to attract new members, sustain participation and earn renewals.

## Solution Messaging
**Create value across the full membership journey**
Use learning to strengthen the member experience from onboarding through certification, professional development and renewal. With flexible, engaging digital learning, associations can help members stay current, build skills and stay connected to their professional community.

## Proof Points / Metrics
- **(ISC)² increased course completion by 25 percentage points**, with 33% of members completing courses, up from the prior year.[file:1]
- **(ISC)² nearly doubled member course participation**, with 27% of members taking at least one course, up from 13% the year before.[file:1]
- **Money Management Institute raised learner engagement from 46% to 87%** across existing programs.[file:1]

## Notes
- Best for association conversations tied to member value, renewal, participation and ongoing professional development.
- Works well when the buyer is trying to connect education investment to stronger member outcomes.

# Boost Productivity

- **Target Persona:** Membership Manager ([[Membership Manager Mo]])
- **Related Use Cases:** [[Content Creation]], [[Process Improvement]]
- **Facilitating Products:** [[Creator+]], [[D2L Link and Lumi]]

## Challenge
Association teams are often asked to do more without adding staff. Manual content work, fragmented systems and repetitive admin tasks can slow down program delivery and make it harder to scale.

## Solution Messaging
**Streamline workflows**
Give teams one place to manage learning from course creation through reporting, with automation, AI-supported workflows and integrations that reduce manual work. This helps staff move faster, support more programs and spend more time on member value instead of administration.

## Proof Points / Metrics
- **Good Roads supports 70+ online courses with just 3 staff and more than 200 volunteer instructors.**[file:1]
- **Gafta saves hours by automating course registration and assignment workflows.**[file:1]
- **Gafta uses Intelligent Agents and release conditions to reduce manual follow-up and certificate administration.**[file:1]

## Notes
- Best for lean association teams focused on efficiency, scale and reducing repetitive admin work.
- Especially relevant when operations depend on volunteers, distributed instructors or small internal teams.

# Generate Non-Dues Revenue

- **Target Persona:** Board Member/Executive ([[Executive Erin]])
- **Related Use Cases:** [[Data-Driven Insights]], [[Monetize Learning]]
- **Facilitating Products:** [[Performance+]], [[D2L Course Merchant]]

## Challenge
Associations need revenue beyond dues to support growth and stay resilient. That requires better visibility into learner demand, stronger program performance and easier ways to commercialize education.

## Solution Messaging
**Turn learning into a growth engine**
Use education to create new revenue streams with ecommerce, stronger program insight and learning experiences members and non-members are willing to pay for. With the right data and delivery model, associations can scale reach, inform new offerings and grow non-dues revenue.

## Proof Points / Metrics
- **Gafta achieved a 49% uplift in learner engagement** and successfully commercialized its training offering.[file:1]
- **Good Roads found a valuable revenue stream through online learning** while engaging new audiences in the private sector and First Nations communities.[file:1]
- **British Society of Lifestyle Medicine increased total income by 46%**, then saw growth rise by more than 120% the following year.[file:1]

## Notes
- Best for executive conversations about growth, diversification and making education a strategic revenue line.
- Useful when the buyer is exploring paid programs for members, partners or broader market audiences.

# Certifications and Credentialing

- **Associated Outcome:** [[Increase Member Engagement and Retention]]
- **Description:** Help associations support professional standards, certifications and continuing professional development with structured learning, assessment and progress tracking.

## Why It Matters
Certification and credentialing programs can deepen member value by helping professionals maintain standards, document progress and advance in their field. They also give associations a durable way to stay relevant across the member lifecycle.

## Proof Points / Metrics
- **Good Roads launched a micro-credential in a matter of weeks**, and it is now used by 109 municipalities.[file:1]
- **IMSA helps reduce certification time** by enabling learners to study and take exams without travel or time away from work.[file:1]
- **CPSA moved courses and exams online**, increasing accessibility and expanding reach for accredited education partners.[file:1]
