# Value Model - Credential Growth

## Use when

The buyer issues certificates, badges, certifications, designations or continuing-education credit and owns a growth or efficiency objective.

## Required inputs

- Eligible population and current registrations.
- Completion, assessment pass, issuance and renewal rates as separate measures.
- Credential price and variable delivery cost for paid programs.
- Administration time per registration, assessment, issuance and renewal.
- Marketing, content, proctoring, verification and support costs.
- Capacity or market constraint the proposed change addresses.

## Formulas

```text
Incremental credentials issued =
  target completions × target pass/issuance rate
  - baseline credentials issued

Incremental credential contribution =
  incremental paid credentials
  × (net realized price - variable delivery cost)
  - incremental program cost

Credential administration capacity =
  annual transactions
  × minutes saved per transaction / 60
  × realization factor
```

Keep issuance volume, earned competence, renewal, revenue and capacity as distinct outcomes.

## Discovery

- What does the credential attest, and who governs validity?
- Which stage limits growth: demand, registration, content, assessment, review, issuance or renewal?
- Is the business objective reach, revenue, member value, compliance or workforce readiness?
- Which records must remain in another system of record?

## Claims to avoid

- More credentials means more competence or career impact.
- Awards issued by the platform are automatically portable, accredited or externally recognized.
- Price multiplied by completions equals profit.
- Brightspace alone satisfies every certification system-of-record requirement.

## Validation

Credential owner validates standards and issuance logic. Finance validates contribution. Technical discovery confirms systems of record, identity, assessment and verification requirements.
