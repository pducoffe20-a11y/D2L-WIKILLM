# Proof Card - ISTE - Community-Focused Professional Learning at Scale

## Context

The International Society for Technology in Education (ISTE) is a nonprofit that helps educators build technology-enabled teaching skills through certification and bite-sized, competency-based courses on Brightspace Core with partner integration.

## Buyer problem

Deliver professional learning and certification to a large, active educator base while sustaining engagement and community connection after the course or certification ends — including during a sudden demand surge (the pandemic).

## What changed

ISTE combined certification pathways with competency-based micro-courses and built communities where educators continue learning from each other after formal coursework is complete.

## Reported outcome

- Helped over 25,000 educators build technology-enabled and online teaching skills at the height of pandemic demand.
- Increased course completion rates by personalizing the learning experience per educator.
- Established peer communities that sustain continuous professional learning after certification.
- Over 30,000 learners served on the platform overall.

## Safe claim

ISTE reports helping more than 25,000 educators strengthen technology-enabled teaching skills during a period of surging demand, with personalization improving completion rates and peer communities sustaining engagement after certification.

## Do not overstate

- Do not cite a specific completion-rate percentage — none was published.
- Do not imply the "25,000 educators at pandemic peak" figure represents steady-state volume.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Education and professional-learning nonprofits
- Associations combining certification with ongoing peer community
- Organizations that need to absorb a sudden spike in training demand

## Discovery questions

- How do you sustain member engagement after a course or certification is completed?
- Could your organization currently absorb a sudden 2-3x increase in training demand?
- How personalized is the learning path for members with different starting skill levels?
