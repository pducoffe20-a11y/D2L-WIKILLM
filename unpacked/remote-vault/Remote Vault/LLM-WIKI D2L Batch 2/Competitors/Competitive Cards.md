# Competitive Cards

These cards are internal decision aids. They do not authorize copying a comparison into buyer-facing material.

## Evidence lanes

| Lane | What it means | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| Current approved guidance | Current material in an approved D2L library, read during this review | Use within the scope and audience stated on the card; recheck volatile claims before external use |
| Historical intelligence | Older competitive research, normally from 2021–2024 | Use to form questions only; do not repeat its feature, pricing, roadmap or vendor claims |
| Automated competitive signal | Official vendor page captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30 | Use as the vendor's current self-positioning; it is not independent proof or approved D2L messaging |

## Governing rule

Start with the buyer's requirements and current-state evidence. A current D2L source can approve a talk track without proving every underlying competitor claim for every edition, package, region or deployment. When the prospect's decision depends on a comparative fact, validate it in the live evaluation.
