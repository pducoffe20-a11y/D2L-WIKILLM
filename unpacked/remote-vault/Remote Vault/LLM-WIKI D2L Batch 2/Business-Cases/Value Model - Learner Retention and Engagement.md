# Value Model - Learner Retention and Engagement

## Use when

The buyer has a defined retention, persistence, completion, renewal or engagement objective and an institutional owner for the measure.

## Measurement chain

Keep each step distinct:

```text
Platform adoption → learning activity → intervention or experience
→ persistence/completion/renewal → financial or mission outcome
```

Evidence at one step does not prove the next.

## Required inputs

- Eligible learner population.
- Baseline and target retention, persistence, completion or renewal rate.
- Definition, cohort and observation window.
- Expected incremental retained learners.
- Buyer-approved net contribution or avoided reacquisition value per retained learner, if monetized.
- Attribution and realization factor reflecting other interventions.
- Incremental program, staffing and support cost.

## Formula

```text
Incremental retained learners =
  eligible population × (target rate - baseline rate)

Risk-adjusted retained contribution =
  incremental retained learners
  × buyer-approved contribution per retained learner
  × attribution/realization factor
  - incremental intervention cost
```

If finance will not validate the unit contribution or attribution, report incremental retained learners as a nonfinancial outcome.

## Discovery

- Which measure is strategically owned: persistence, completion, renewal or something else?
- What cohort and period define the baseline?
- Which interventions accompany the technology change?
- How will the buyer distinguish correlation from contribution?
- What value does finance recognize per retained learner?

## Proof

[[LLM-WIKI D2L/05-Customer-Proof 1/Proof Card - SAIT - Analytics-Led LMS Adoption]] reports a 15-percentage-point increase in instructor engagement after analytics, integration, leadership goals and peer support. It does not prove student retention or a platform-only effect.

## Claims to avoid

- Engagement causes retention.
- LMS activity alone explains learner outcomes.
- A percentage-point change from another institution is a forecast input.
- Tuition, dues or program price equals retained contribution.
