# Source - Course Merchant Overview

## Seller truth

Course Merchant is positioned for branded online course discovery, registration, payment, automated enrolment, and SSO into Brightspace, particularly when external learners need access.

## Evidence

- The page marks the product generally available and lists regional and language scope.
- Qualification signals include users without active Brightspace accounts, paid courses, and a branded ecommerce experience.
- Association and training-organization profiles are explicitly included in the ICP table.

## Objections and cautions

- ICP criteria can change.
- Region, language, implementation, authentication, accessibility, and packaging details require current confirmation.
- Do not infer pricing or entitlement.
