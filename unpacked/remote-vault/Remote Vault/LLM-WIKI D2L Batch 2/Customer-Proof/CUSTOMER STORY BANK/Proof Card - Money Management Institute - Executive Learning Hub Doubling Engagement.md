# Proof Card - Money Management Institute - Executive Learning Hub Doubling Engagement

## Context

The Money Management Institute (MMI) is a trade association for the financial-services industry. It launched the MMI Learning Hub on Brightspace Core — a centralized, hybrid, cohort-based ecosystem supporting three leadership programs across early-, mid-, and executive-level professionals from more than 200 member firms.

## Buyer problem

A trade association needs to deliver differentiated leadership development to professionals at different career stages, across a large multi-firm membership, while proving the program is worth members' time.

## What changed

MMI centralized three leadership programs into one hybrid, cohort-based learning hub with data-driven insights and secure collaboration across participating firms.

## Reported outcome

- 95%+ learner engagement consistently achieved in the Executive IQ program.
- Learner engagement scores for existing programs nearly doubled, from 46% to 87%.
- 98% of learners said they'd recommend the program to a colleague.

## Safe claim

MMI reports that consolidating its leadership programs onto Brightspace nearly doubled learner engagement scores (46% to 87%) and sustained 95%+ engagement in its Executive IQ program, with 98% of learners willing to recommend it.

## Do not overstate

- Do not imply the 46%→87% jump is attributable to Brightspace alone — program redesign was part of the change.
- Do not promise a specific engagement percentage for a different program structure.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Trade and professional associations with tiered (early/mid/executive) development programs
- Multi-firm or multi-employer membership models
- Associations wanting to prove program value with hard engagement data

## Discovery questions

- How do you currently measure engagement across your professional development programs, and what's the baseline?
- Do you offer differentiated programming for different career stages, and is it on one platform or several?
- What would a large jump in engagement scores be worth to your board or member-firm relationships?
