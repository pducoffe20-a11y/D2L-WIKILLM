# Evidence contract

Use this ordering:

`verified fact → signal → interpretation → why it matters → seller decision → next action → desired buyer outcome`

Rules:

- Search the vault first.
- After opening the strongest vault evidence, invoke the vendored `last30days` skill for substantive seller answers.
- Reconcile public findings with the vault without changing note status or verification scope.
- Treat vendor claims and community engagement as signals, not approved proof or buyer intent.
- Prefer current verified knowledge; label reviewed, disputed, stale, restricted, and inbox evidence.
- Treat authority and freshness separately.
- Preserve contradictions.
- Missing or inaccessible evidence is unknown.
- Slack activity is not buyer engagement or approved messaging.
- Jira can establish implementation reality, not approved external language.
- Do not infer pricing, packaging, roadmap, legal terms, CRM stage, amount, probability, ownership, or dates.
- Never silently overwrite reviewed or verified knowledge.
- External writes require separate explicit authorization.
