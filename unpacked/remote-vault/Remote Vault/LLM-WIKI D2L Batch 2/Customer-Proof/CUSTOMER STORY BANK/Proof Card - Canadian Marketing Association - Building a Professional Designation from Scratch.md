# Proof Card - Canadian Marketing Association - Building a Professional Designation from Scratch

## Context

The Canadian Marketing Association (CMA) worked with D2L (Brightspace Core, Technical Support Services, Learning Services, Partner Integration) to launch the Chartered Marketer (CM) designation — a brand-new professional credential, not a migration of an existing one.

## Buyer problem

An association wants to launch a new professional designation that gives members a differentiated credential and career pathway, but needs a platform and delivery partner to build the program from the ground up.

## What changed

CMA partnered with D2L experts to design and launch the CM designation, giving learners flexible, anytime-anywhere access to balance study with work.

## Reported outcome

- Designation has helped advance careers — multiple learners reported promotions after achieving it.
- Anytime, anywhere access let learners balance study with full-time work.
- More than 400 marketers have received, applied for, or are working toward the designation.

## Safe claim

CMA reports that its Chartered Marketer designation, built and delivered on Brightspace, has been pursued by more than 400 marketers, with participants reporting career advancement including promotions.

## Do not overstate

- Do not claim a specific promotion rate or percentage — "many learners" is qualitative, not quantified in the source.
- Do not imply this designation launched instantly; it required Learning Services partnership to design from scratch.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Associations considering launching a brand-new credential (not modernizing an existing one)
- Buyers evaluating D2L Learning Services for program design support, not just the LMS
- Career-advancement-focused member value propositions

## Discovery questions

- Are you looking to modernize an existing credential, or build a new one from scratch?
- How much in-house instructional design capacity do you have for building a new designation?
- What career outcomes would make a new credential worth the investment for your members?
