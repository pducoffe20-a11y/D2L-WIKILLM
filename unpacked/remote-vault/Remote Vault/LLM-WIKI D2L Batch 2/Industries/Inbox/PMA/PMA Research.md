## Executive Summary

**Precision Metalforming Association (PMA)** is a trade association serving the precision metalforming industry. Mary Pramik, as Workforce Development Director, owns PMA's apprenticeship and certification programs—a critical function given the metalforming industry's severe skills shortage.

**D2L Fit: HIGH**

PMA represents a textbook D2L association opportunity:
1. **Non-Dues Revenue Growth** - Training programs are revenue lever; currently limited by in-person delivery model
2. **Workforce Development Mission** - Industry skills gap demands scaled, accessible training delivery
3. **Operational Efficiency** - Workforce dev team likely stretched thin managing course dev, enrollment, compliance
4. **Member Engagement** - Quality, modern training programs drive member satisfaction and retention

**Opportunity Summary:**
- **Revenue Opportunity:** Scale non-dues training revenue by 40-60% through online/hybrid delivery = $250K-$600K incremental revenue potential
- **Efficiency Opportunity:** Reduce course dev time by 30% and admin overhead by 40% through automation
- **Mission Opportunity:** Expand apprenticeship reach beyond geography and shift constraints through digital delivery

**Expected Sales Cycle:** 3-6 months | **Implementation:** 4-8 weeks (pilot), 3-4 months (full rollout)
