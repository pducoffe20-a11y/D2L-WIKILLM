## Where they fit

Litmos may fit corporate buyers seeking a fast-to-deploy LMS for employee, partner, or customer training with heavy emphasis on AI-driven "in the flow of work" microlearning and a large pre-built content library. Confirm actual audience, scale, and whether the buyer values embedded AI workflow triggers specifically.

## Likely buyer rationale

- Fast time-to-value and "operational simplicity" positioning.
- Interest in AI-assisted content creation and contextual/microlearning delivery.
- Need to train multiple distinct audiences (employees, partners, customers) from one platform.
- Preference for a content-library-inclusive LMS over building courses from scratch.

## Discovery questions

- Is the priority employee development, partner/channel enablement, customer education, or a mix — and does the buyer need role-based pathways across all three?
- How will "AI-triggered microlearning in the flow of work" actually integrate with the buyer's existing tools, and what does that require operationally?
- What certification, compliance, and audit-readiness requirements exist, and how deep does the buyer need reporting to go?
- What data, analytics, and executive-visibility requirements exist beyond completions?
- How complex is the buyer's administration model (delegated admin, multiple audiences, integrations)?
- What migration or content-authoring investment already exists that would carry over or be lost?

## Safe differentiation

- Probe whether "learning acceleration" claims translate into measurable outcomes the buyer can validate, not just marketing language.
- Test platform depth for regulated, competency-based, or multi-audience programs — a content-library-centric LMS is not automatically equivalent to a learning platform built for complex program design.
- Demonstrate Brightspace against the buyer's actual audience, workflow, and reporting requirements rather than generic AI claims.
- Use relevant D2L customer proof only after matching industry, use case, and outcome — do not apply financial-services or association proof points universally.

## Claims to avoid

- Litmos lacks AI capability, integrations, or a content library (their site demonstrates otherwise).
- Litmos is only for simple/basic training — their positioning explicitly targets partner and customer education at scale.
- D2L deploys faster, costs less, or produces stronger ROI without a validated basis.
- Vendor-reported customer counts (11,000+ orgs, 150 countries) or AI claims from Litmos's own site as independent proof.
- Any claim about current corporate parent/ownership without verifying it first.
