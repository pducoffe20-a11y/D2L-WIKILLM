# D2L Lumi Public Capabilities

The current public page organizes D2L Lumi around educators, course authors, learners and leadership.

## Public capability themes

- Generate video captions.
- Align learning materials with defined outcomes.
- Generate assignment, discussion, activity and quiz ideas from existing content.
- Create practice questions and knowledge checks.
- Produce module summaries.
- Generate learning materials and interactive course elements from existing files.
- Provide an on-demand learning-assistant experience.
- Generate educator-directed feedback.
- Surface learner-performance insights.
- Support multilingual content described on the page in French, Spanish, Portuguese and Dutch.

## Boundaries

- The Brightspace overview presents Lumi as an optional add-on.
- Individual Lumi functions may have distinct release, entitlement, language and regional availability.
- AI output remains subject to educator or organizational review; do not claim autonomous accuracy or guaranteed outcomes.
- The public page offers a 30-day trial; a trial is not evidence of contracted entitlement.
