## Strategic Positioning: Why D2L Owns Tech Council LMS

### The Opportunity
Tech councils and professional associations in the technology sector represent a **critical but underserved segment** for LMS modernization. They sit at the intersection of:
- **Membership associations** (community, engagement, dues)
- **Professional development** (certifications, skills training, CE credits)
- **Revenue growth** (non-dues revenue from training/certs is 15–30% of operating budget for mature associations)
- **Operational inefficiency** (fragmented platforms, manual workflows, low completion rates)

### Why D2L Wins vs. Alternatives

#### vs. Moodle (Free Open-Source)
**Tech councils' situation:** "We run on Moodle—it's free, but our IT team spends 20+ hours/week maintaining it"

**D2L Take:**
> "Moodle is a great platform, but it's designed for institutions, not associations. You're not running an academic calendar. You need:
> - **Certification workflows** (not just courses) – automated course sequences, credential issuance, transcript management, CE credit tracking
> - **Member integration** – one-click member enrollment, AMS sync, member profile data in learning platform
> - **Speed to revenue** – launch new credentials in 7 weeks, not 12
> 
> Moodle requires a developer to do any of that. D2L comes pre-built for your model. And your IT team can focus on member infrastructure instead of platform maintenance."

**Proof Point:** ISC2 (runs cybersecurity certs at scale), Canadian Marketing Association (launched credential in months, not a year)

## Selling Strategy: Tech Council Playbook

### Phase 1: Qualifying the Opportunity (Week 1)
**Goal:** Confirm fit and identify buying committee  
**Stakeholder:** Typically VP Education or Learning Director first  
**Key Question:** "Walk me through your credential programs. How many members do you serve annually?"

**Listen for:**
- ✓ 2+ credential programs running today (signals non-dues revenue model)
- ✓ Completion rates below 60% (pain signal for revenue impact)
- ✓ Course development timeline > 10 weeks (efficiency opportunity)
- ✓ Mentions of platform fragmentation or IT overhead

**Red flags:**
- ✗ Associations with only 1 credential program (smaller scale, lower deal size)
- ✗ Entirely volunteer-run (no budget authority)
- ✗ Currently on a well-maintained LMS with no pain (lower priority)

---

### Phase 2: Building the Business Case (Weeks 2–3)
**Goal:** Quantify impact, identify full buying committee  
**Stakeholders:** VP Education + CFO + CIO
