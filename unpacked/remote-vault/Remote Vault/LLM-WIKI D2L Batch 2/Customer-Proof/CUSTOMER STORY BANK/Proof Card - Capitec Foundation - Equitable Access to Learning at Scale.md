# Proof Card - Capitec Foundation - Equitable Access to Learning at Scale

## Context

The Capitec Foundation champions maths education access in South Africa, using Brightspace Core with Performance+ and Optimization Services to reach roughly 8,000 students through technology hubs regardless of geography or social mobility.

## Buyer problem

A foundation or nonprofit needs to deliver credible, trackable learning outcomes to underresourced or geographically dispersed populations, and prove impact through analytics rather than anecdote.

## What changed

Capitec Foundation deployed easy-access digital learning at technology hubs and used learning analytics to track student progress at scale.

## Reported outcome

- Easy-access, digitally enabled learning delivered through technology hubs.
- Student progress tracked through learning analytics (Performance+).
- Learning made accessible regardless of geography, demographics, or social mobility.
- Rapid student onboarding supporting ambitious growth plans.

## Safe claim

Capitec Foundation reports using Brightspace and Performance+ to deliver maths education to roughly 8,000 students across South Africa through technology hubs, with analytics tracking progress and supporting equitable access regardless of geography or social mobility.

## Do not overstate

- Do not cite specific learning-outcome gains (test scores, pass rates) — none were published in this record.
- Do not claim the model generalizes without technology-hub infrastructure already in place.
- Recheck the live source before quoting exact language externally.

## Best fit

- Foundations and nonprofits with an equity/access mission
- Programs delivered through community or technology hubs rather than 1:1 devices
- Buyers who need learning analytics to prove impact to funders or boards

## Discovery questions

- How do you currently prove program impact to your board or funders?
- Is your delivery model hub-based, 1:1, or a mix — and how does that affect platform requirements?
- What does "equitable access" mean in measurable terms for your organization?
