## Signal

- Published story summary: Before 2022, State University of New York (SUNY) had a problem—each campus had its own learning management system (LMS). For the largest university system in the United States, it was a big problem. Imagine juggling multiple logins, different systems and inconsistent experiences across campuses. That was life for SUNY leaders, faculty and students. Data was scattered, support varied and security was patchy. SUNY needed to unify and simplify the learning experience for everyone. The Solution? Transforming the chaos…
- Reported impact: SUNY System implemented a cohesive Brightspace ecosystem for 58 campuses across three instances, with 56 campuses in one multi-tenant Brightspace instance.
- Reported impact: SUNY has standardized the LMS interface and navigation and centralized the information students and faculty require for SUNY courses across all campuses and modalities.
- Reported impact: SUNY’s DLE enables the SUNY System to provide all campuses with consistent, robust LMS functionality while offering new opportunities for cross-campus collaboration and insights.
- Reported impact: Implementing the D2L Brightspace environment has reduced LMS license costs for SUNY campuses and implementation costs per tenant.
