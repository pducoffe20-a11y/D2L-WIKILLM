# Industry Playbooks

The dedicated playbooks combine current, reputable industry evidence with clearly labeled seller interpretation. Use external sources to frame discovery—not to claim buyer intent, D2L fit or a product outcome.

## Playbook routes

- [[Corporate Learning Playbook]] — workforce skill change, reskilling, AI literacy, onboarding and consolidation
- [[Higher Education Playbook]] — distance education, institutional trust, faculty adoption, student experience and migration
- [[K-12 Playbook]] — learning recovery, intervention, teacher capacity, family engagement and district consistency
- [[Government and Public Sector Playbook]] — workforce AI literacy, role segmentation, governance, procurement and audit evidence
- [[Healthcare and Regulated Training Playbook]] — workforce pressure, compliance, clinical learning, CPD, privacy and blended delivery

## Source standard

This set prioritizes the World Economic Forum, U.S. National Center for Education Statistics, EDUCAUSE, OECD and World Health Organization. Each dedicated note includes:

- verified sector pressures
- likely buyers and triggers as hypotheses
- priority use cases
- relevant customer proof or a visible proof gap
- discovery questions
- objection guidance
- safe-claim boundaries
- business cases with required inputs, formulas and claim limits

## Common playbook rule

External industry evidence supports a question or hypothesis. It does not prove that a specific account shares the condition, that D2L is the required solution, or that a customer outcome will repeat.
