# Proof Card - REINSW - Rapid Implementation Driving Provider Growth

## Context

The Real Estate Institute of New South Wales (REINSW) is a professional/compliance-training association serving 3,500-5,000 learners. It adopted Brightspace Core in February 2023 to modernize training delivery and reporting.

## Buyer problem

Compliance-driven training providers need fast time-to-value and clear visibility into learner performance to justify the switch and keep serving members without disruption.

## What changed

REINSW implemented Brightspace in a 12-week period and used its visual reporting features to let support staff engage learners with more targeted support.

## Reported outcome

- Swift implementation completed within 12 weeks.
- Reported growth in the number of people using REINSW as a training provider (exact figure requires source recheck).
- Reported improvement in learner satisfaction (exact figure requires source recheck).
- Visual reporting enabled more targeted staff engagement with learners.

## Safe claim

REINSW reports implementing Brightspace in 12 weeks and using its visual reporting to give staff more targeted visibility into learner performance, supporting growth in training-provider usage and learner satisfaction.
