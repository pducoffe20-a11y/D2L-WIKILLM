# Brightspace Mobile and Pulse

## Brightspace Pulse

- Visual timeline for upcoming assignments, quizzes, discussions and announcements.
- Notifications for new content, due dates and activity updates.
- Mobile access to grades and feedback.
- Offline access to non-HTML course content.
- Single sign-on into the full Brightspace experience.

## Responsive platform

The page says Brightspace adapts to different screen sizes and emphasizes mobile-first workflows, progressive disclosure and accessibility-informed design. It also describes uploading audio and video from a phone.

## Additional audience

The page includes a Parent & Guardian App for family visibility into learner activity.

## Boundaries

- Offline behavior is specifically described for non-HTML content; do not generalize it to every Brightspace activity.
- Confirm current iOS, Android, notification and Parent & Guardian availability before committing to a workflow.
