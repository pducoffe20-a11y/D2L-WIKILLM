# Video Assignments and Virtual Classroom

## Public capabilities

- Individual and interactive video assignments for synchronous or asynchronous learning.
- Learner recording and submission with time-stamped feedback.
- Self-assessment, coaching and peer review.
- Live video conferencing and two-way interaction within Brightspace.
- Scheduling, recording and archiving virtual-classroom sessions.
- Repeated practice and video-based demonstration of skills.

## Premium boundaries

The page assigns additional capabilities to Premium, including Q&A and group assignments, interactive video, AI-based coaching feedback, additional virtual-classroom participants, longer sessions and unlimited storage.

## Boundary rule

Do not present Premium-only functionality as part of the standard experience. Confirm current participant, duration, storage and AI entitlements.
