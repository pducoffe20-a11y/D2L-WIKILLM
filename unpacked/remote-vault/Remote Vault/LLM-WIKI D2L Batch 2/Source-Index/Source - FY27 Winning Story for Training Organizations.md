# Source - FY27 Winning Story for Training Organizations

## Seller truth

A winning story should connect a buyer-relevant differentiator to appropriately matched customer proof and validated metrics.

## Evidence

- The deck distinguishes the general D2L story from a buyer-specific winning story.
- It instructs sellers to select proof that matches the buyer’s industry, geography, challenge, and desired outcome.
- It identifies Modern States administration and program outcomes as proof candidates.

## Objections and cautions

- Validate every customer metric against the underlying approved customer-metrics table before external use.
- Keep Modern States outcomes scoped to Modern States; one example does not establish a universal outcome.
- Do not reuse older third-party market statistics as current evidence.
- Partnership language in this enablement deck is positioning, not a universal verified fact.
