# Competitive Card - Docebo

## Governance

- **Current approved guidance:** the [July 2026 Financial Services ICP PMM Playbook](https://d2lmail.sharepoint.com/sites/ProductMarketingAssets/Corporate/Financial%20Services%20PMM%20Playbook.pdf?web=1) positions Docebo around AI-powered learning, content discovery and customer education. It suggests differentiating through regulated learning, certifications and role-based workforce development. This is financial-services guidance, not a universal battlecard.
- **Historical intelligence:** Docebo product, analytics and feature-comparison material in the historical CI inventory was last edited in 2021–2022.
- **Automated competitive signal:** [Docebo's official learning-platform page](https://www.docebo.com/learning-platform/), captured with Crawlberg on 2026-07-30, presents one platform for employees, customers and partners, with skills intelligence, content, analytics, integrations and multiple corporate use cases.
- **Missing evidence:** no current dedicated D2L-vs-Docebo battlecard was found in the approved library.

## Where they fit

Docebo may fit enterprise buyers that want centralized learning for several audiences and place value on AI, content discovery, skills intelligence, customer education or partner enablement.

## Likely buyer rationale

- Consolidating employee, customer and partner learning.
- Using learning to support customer retention, enablement or revenue.
- Interest in AI-led personalization and skills.
- Preference for a corporate-learning platform with a broad use-case story.

## Discovery questions

- Which audience owns the business case, and which audiences are secondary?
- Are regulated learning and certification requirements central or incidental?
- How are skills, roles and learning outcomes defined and governed?
- What customer-education, partner or commerce workflows are required?
- Which analytics must connect learning activity to business outcomes?
- What content, integrations and services are included in the proposed scope?

## Safe differentiation

- In regulated contexts, test evidence, recertification, role-based pathways and reporting in detail.
- Compare the end-to-end learning workflow and buyer outcome, not the number of products named.
- Demonstrate Brightspace personalization, content, analytics and multi-audience operation against agreed scenarios.
- Validate proposed packages and services on both sides.

## Claims to avoid

- Docebo cannot support compliance, certification, role-based learning or enterprise scale.
- Its AI, analytics, implementation or support is inferior.
- D2L is more unified without mapping the buyer's actual solution architecture.
- Vendor customer stories or self-described differentiators as independent proof.
- Applying a financial-services differentiator to unrelated industries without discovery.

## Desired next step

Choose one primary audience, one cross-audience workflow and one regulated or skills scenario, then score both proposed solutions against the same evidence.
