# Services and Implementation Positioning


    ## Seller truth

    Services scope and implementation behavior must come from current approved documentation and named owners.

    ## Why it matters

    Prevents commitments based on old enablement or field shorthand.

    ## When to use it

    Solution design, proposal, and handoff.

    ## Evidence

    Batch 1 evidence supports this as a seller discovery and qualification pattern for associations. It does not prove that the condition exists for a specific buyer. Use the association pitch deck, healthcare-association collateral, Product Resources hub, Course Merchant overview, and Corporate Use Cases page according to their authority and freshness.

    ## Seller interpretation

    **Interpretation:** Live Rovo and SharePoint checks are required before external statements.

    ## Talk track

    I’ll confirm the current delivery model and ownership before we turn this into a commitment.

    ## Discovery questions

    - What buyer outcome requires services?
- Which responsibilities need explicit ownership?
- What must be true before launch?

    ## Objections and cautions

    Never infer scope, hours, dates, staffing, or contractual commitments.
