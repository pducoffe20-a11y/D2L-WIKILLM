# Learning Administration Manager

## Seller truth

The buyer evaluating whether to offload user, role, permission, configuration, course, integration, and data-administration work to a named D2L Learning Administration Manager (LAM) resource, rather than staffing it internally. Service availability, packaging, and region must be checked against the current deck before quoting specifics.

## Why it matters

Persona notes guide discovery without inventing priorities. This persona's core question is build-vs-buy for administrative capacity — directly relevant to the "Implementation Capacity" and "Administration Effort" objections, and to organizations too lean to staff a dedicated internal administrator (see the PDI proof card for a comparable small-team capacity story).

## When to use it

Conversations where the buyer has expressed limited internal staffing for platform administration, or where a deal is stalling on "who will actually run this day-to-day."

## Evidence

The LAM pitch deck (source_url above) documents that a named LAM resource can support user, role, permission, configuration, course, integration, and data-administration work. This is source-bounded guidance — service availability, package, and region must be verified in the current deck, not assumed.

## Seller interpretation

**Interpretation:** This persona often surfaces after — not before — the Administration Effort or Implementation Capacity objection is raised. It's a resolution path (managed service), not a standalone discovery starting point.

## Talk track

If administrative capacity is the constraint, would it help to talk through what a managed administration resource could take off your team's plate?

## Discovery questions

- Is your constraint a lack of internal staff, a lack of specific platform expertise, or both?
- Have you used a managed-services model for other systems before, and how did that go?
- What administrative tasks would you most want to hand off versus keep in-house?
- What's your comfort level with an external resource having configuration access?

## Objections and cautions

Titles do not prove authority, budget, or ownership. Do not quote specific LAM service package details, pricing, or regional availability without checking the current deck — packaging changes over time and this note does not carry that detail.
