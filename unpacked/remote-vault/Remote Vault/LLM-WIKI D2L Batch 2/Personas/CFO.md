# Chief Financial Officer / VP Finance & Operations

## Role context

**Demographics & titles:**
- Chief Financial Officer
- VP of Finance & Administration
- Director of Operations

**Reports to:** CEO / Board of Directors

**Scope:** Financial strategy, operational efficiency, risk management, cost control

## Business drivers

**Primary objective:** Protect margins, maximize non-dues revenue ROI, ensure predictable SaaS operational costs, and mitigate compliance/security risk.

**Key performance indicators (KPIs):**
- Payback period
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
- Gross/Net Revenue from CE offerings
- Cost per Active Learner

**Business pressures:**
- Unpredictable open-source/legacy maintenance costs
- Risk of data breaches and regulatory fines
- Board demand for positive margins on training programs

## Buying behavior

**Technical literacy:** Low to moderate; focuses on financial models and risk mitigation

**Decision role:** Final Economic Approver

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Financial ROI model
- Predictable subscription pricing vs. hidden implementation fees
- SOC2 Type II / ISO 27001 compliance

**Key objections & concerns:**
- What is the true Total Cost of Ownership?
- How long before we break even?
- Why pay for enterprise LMS when open-source looks 'free'?

## Discovery questions

- If we could reduce your instructional design cycle by 30% while eliminating third-party authoring license fees, what impact would that have on your margin?
- What are your current hidden costs with maintaining open-source or legacy platforms?
- How does CE program revenue compare to your total operating costs today?

## D2L proof points

**Value propositions:**
- Eliminates hidden maintenance fees associated with open-source tools (plugins, custom coding, patches)
- 30% reduction in course development time using Creator+ native authoring tools

**Relevant data points:**
- Predictable monthly/annual SaaS subscription model
- Included platform updates and security patches
- Transparent pricing with no surprise implementation costs

## Key messaging

*The hidden costs of 'free' open-source tools often exceed enterprise SaaS subscription prices. D2L's managed platform eliminates unpredictable maintenance and allows your team to focus on revenue-generating programs.*
