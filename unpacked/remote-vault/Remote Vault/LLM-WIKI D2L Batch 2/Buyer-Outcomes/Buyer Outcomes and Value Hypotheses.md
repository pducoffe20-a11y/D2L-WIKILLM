## Use rule

Revenue growth, learner retention, administrative savings, time to launch, credential growth, risk reduction, consolidation and global scale remain hypotheses until validated for a buyer. [[Business Case Toolkit]] now provides governed calculation structures for those outcomes. The presence of a model does not verify that D2L will produce the outcome.

Use buyer actuals, buyer-approved forecasts, validated workflows and relevant proof. Do not convert source-stated themes or customer-specific results into ROI, savings, causality or timeline claims without buyer-specific evidence.
