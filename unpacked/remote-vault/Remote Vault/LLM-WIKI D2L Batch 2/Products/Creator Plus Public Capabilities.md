# Creator Plus Public Capabilities

Creator+ is presented as a Brightspace course-authoring add-on.

## Public capabilities

- More than 50 H5P interactive content types integrated with Brightspace.
- Drag-and-drop creation of activities such as polls, games, videos and quizzes.
- Prebuilt templates and global styles for consistent branding.
- AI-assisted generation of interactive content from audio, video, URLs or text.
- Advanced reporting and analytics for interactive learning content.
- Gradable H5P activities connected to the Brightspace gradebook.
- Authoring within the Brightspace editor without requiring coding or design expertise.

## Boundaries

- Creator+ is an optional Brightspace extension.
- H5P content types and AI features may have their own availability and entitlement requirements.
- Do not translate faster authoring positioning into a customer-specific time or cost result.
