# Course Merchant Public Capabilities

Course Merchant is presented as a Brightspace ecommerce add-on.

## Public capabilities

- Branded and customizable course storefront and catalog.
- Seat limits and waitlists.
- Discount codes, multiseat purchases, bundles, membership discounts and early-bird pricing.
- More than 30 supported payment gateways, including card payments, PayPal and offline invoicing.
- Free course offerings.
- Shopper self-registration, instant enrollment and single sign-on into Brightspace.
- Individual or bulk allocation of seats purchased for groups.

## Boundaries

- Course Merchant is an add-on rather than a universal Brightspace Core capability.
- Payment gateway, region, currency, language, security and implementation fit require current qualification.
- The public page establishes capability availability, not revenue or conversion outcomes.
