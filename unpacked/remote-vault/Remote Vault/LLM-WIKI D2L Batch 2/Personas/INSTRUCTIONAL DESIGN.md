# eLearning Manager / Director of Instructional Design

## Role context

**Demographics & titles:**
- Learning Experience Designer
- Manager of Digital Education
- Instructional Technologist

**Reports to:** VP of Education / Chief Learning Officer

**Scope:** Course design and development, learning experience optimization, content management, instructional strategy

## Business drivers

**Primary objective:** Build engaging, interactive, accessible learning content quickly without heavy coding.

**Key performance indicators (KPIs):**
- Course release velocity
- Learner engagement/satisfaction scores (CSAT/NPS)
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance rate

**Business pressures:**
- Small team converting static PDFs to interactive courses
- Multi-device/mobile compatibility requirements
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA) for regulatory and member satisfaction

## Buying behavior

**Technical literacy:** Moderate to high; familiar with authoring tools, SCORM, xAPI, but not necessarily full-stack developers

**Decision role:** Key Influencer & End-User Champion

**Primary concerns:**
- Usability of the authoring interface
- Ability to import existing content (SCORM packages)
- Development speed and efficiency
- Team training time required

**Key objections & concerns:**
- Is the course builder rigid?
- Can we import existing SCORM packages easily?
- Will this slow down our instructional design workflow?

## Discovery questions

- How much time does your team currently spend on course development, from design to deployment?
- What percentage of your courses are created from scratch vs. adapted from existing content?
- What accessibility compliance issues or member complaints have you encountered with your current tools?
