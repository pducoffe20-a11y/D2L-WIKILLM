# Brightspace Public Product Overview

## Verified platform statements

The public Brightspace page describes Brightspace as a learning management platform that combines artificial intelligence, learning science and human ingenuity. It presents these platform-level jobs:

- Create and deliver interactive, engaging and accessible learning content.
- Personalize learning experiences at scale.
- Track trends and support data-informed decisions with analytics.
- Serve higher education, K-12, training organizations, associations, businesses and government.

The public D2L Products page also describes automation of routine tasks, learning delivery across devices, educator-focused tools, analytics and inclusive learning.

## Public add-on map

The Brightspace page identifies these optional extensions:

- [[D2L Lumi Public Capabilities]] — AI-enabled assistance.
- [[Creator Plus Public Capabilities]] — course authoring and interactive content.
- [[Achievement Plus Public Capabilities]] — learning outcomes and achievement reporting.
- [[Performance Plus Public Capabilities]] — learning analytics.
- [[Course Merchant Public Capabilities]] — ecommerce and self-enrollment.
- [[D2L Link Public Integrations]] — system integration.

## Services

The public product pages link Brightspace with Learning Services, Onboarding Services, Customer Support Services and Optimization Services. This establishes that D2L offers service options; it does not establish customer-specific scope, price or timeline.

## Boundaries

- Add-ons are not automatically part of Brightspace Core.
- Product-page language is public positioning, not a customer-specific outcome guarantee.
- Animated customer counters were malformed in the extracted page and were deliberately excluded.
